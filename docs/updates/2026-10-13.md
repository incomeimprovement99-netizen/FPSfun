**Up onto THE TERMINAL's roofs.** Two jump pads in front of the station throw you up onto its curved glass-vault roofs,
with AC boxes up there for cover. Inside, the concourse now has ticket machines, vending machines and benches to fight
around.

**THE STACKS, the north-west district, is open.** Three towers of stacked capsule pods now stand past MOTEL HILL, the
tallest two of them joined on one podium with THE STACKS in pink neon over its stair. In front is an avenue of cypresses
through a car park, with kiosks and crates to fight round. The capsule hotel you walk through, and the ways up onto the
pods, come next.

**The pistols sit where Apex holds its Wingman.** APUHTHEE was out to the right; it now sits low in the middle-right of
the screen, in both hands, as Apex's Wingman does, and STRYDER comes a little nearer. Aimed, APUHTHEE no longer fills the
middle of the screen with the back of the gun: it is held out at arm's length, its sight small in the middle with your
hands under it.

**The capsule hotel opens in THE STACKS.** In the north-west corner, 21 concrete capsule pods are stacked three storeys
high round a court, with open galleries in front of every floor and stairs up to a railed roof deck. Fight pod to pod,
storey by storey, with crates in every pod and AC boxes on the roof. CAPSULE HOTEL glows pink over the court.

**High ground in THE STACKS, and the capsule hotel lit up.** A jump pad by the road throws you onto a railed pod roof

its pods light up as you come near.

**GLASS HEIGHTS rises in the north, and the High City bridges are easier.** Past the ring road to the north, four white
glass towers now carry their decks at the High City's height round a courtyard. A 72 m black glass tower with a cyan

the deck level from the centre into the new district. And you no longer have to hop onto the High City's bridges: their
stairs were a step too high at the top, and now you walk straight up.

**The USSO's left hand is on the gun.** It used to hang off to the left of the gun, cupped round the corner with only
the fingertips touching. Now the palm sits flat against the gun's left side, the fingers wrap under its front and the
thumb lies along the side.

**A glass lift up GLASS HEIGHTS.** A glass lift in GLASS HEIGHTS' courtyard now carries you on a cyan rope up onto the
white glass decks. Cover stands on the decks and round the courtyard below. And the stairs off the Sky Ring lifts'
landings got the same fix as the bridges: you walk straight up them now.

**THE STRIP, in the east.** The east side of the city has its first district. An 87 m black tower stands on the
city's edge at the end of a boulevard, its foot ringed by glowing red blocks, with THE STRIP in magenta over the
gate. A glowing pyramid fills the plaza beside it. You can run straight up any of its faces onto a lookout
7 m up.

**Trigger fingers on the triggers.** APUHTHEE, PANDA and CHOOCH now rest the right forefinger on the trigger; CHOOCH's
hand had been holding the bottom of its grip, well behind it. BIGANTLER followed; ANAKIN and REZ are next.

**REZ's trigger finger, and ANAKIN's reload you can see.** REZ now rests its forefinger on the trigger, with the hand
