# 2026-11-06

**No more blank signs in THE TERMINAL and THE STACKS.** The metro map boards in THE TERMINAL's halls had a plain white back that showed whenever you looked along a hall; each now stands back to back with a second board, so you see a map from either side. THE STACKS' capsule kiosk swaps its flat pink windows for a lit advert.

**THE CANYON's banners.** The lines of cloth strung across THE CANYON's market street were flat coloured squares from a distance; each now carries one of the city's posters, so they read as hung banners over the stalls.

**Each district's entrance in its own style.** Where the ring road meets each district, the cover there now comes from that district: LANTERN ALLEYS' entrance has its brick walls instead of grey concrete and white pods, THE WORKS' its rusted panels, THE STACKS' its cooling units and THE TERRACES' its hedges.
