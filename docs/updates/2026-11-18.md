# 2026-11-18

**Batch 14 (map version 147): the whole inner ring rebuilt the way the north-east was, each quarter its own way.**

- **Every quarter now has ways up with no pad:** the high bridges between the 27 m blocks come down to 12 to 20 metres in
  each quarter, broken over the streets and stepped up to the blocks. From the street to a block's roof takes 9 to 14
  seconds on foot, to each of the four tall blocks from both of its sides.
- **Each quarter plays differently.** The north-west's bridge runs high on one side and low on the other, and the way up
  starts with a stair in MOTEL HILL's alley. The south-east's is broken three ways round THE WELL, with a dip down onto
  its terrace. MARKET's stays at full height off the south block and then drops 14 metres to a gallery, and MARKET keeps
  its stall line down the middle of its streets with a lane either side. The north-east got the decks the reviewers
  asked for.
- **Cover over the fast path:** decks across the streets at about 9 metres, many with a second floor at 12.5. The
  middle height the reviewers said was missing (roofs between 12 and 20 metres) is four times what it was across the
  ring, and the streets are seen from fewer high spots in every quarter.
- **Walls to run:** each quarter has a run of 30 metres or more along walls, ending on a roof or a terrace.
- **Found by the bake and mended:** a shop sign that landed on a block's climbing ledges, street clutter landing in
  MARKET's lanes (it now keeps out of them), the north-west gap's pads standing against a rail, and a ring road corner
  left with no cover. The details are Milestone 1181.
- **Next:** the third layout review, with fresh reviewers given the new numbers and asked to compare the four quarters
  and judge the matches that end in the outer sectors as well as the centre.

**Later the same day: the third layout review, and the ending's rule.**

- **The review:** two fresh reviewers scored the map 5.5 of 10 for play (4.5 before batch 14). Flow and height are up to
  6. Their first answers on the ending measured the wrong spots, because the brief said the zone opens "at the sector's
  middle" and they took the middle of its rectangle, on the ring road; the zones really open on the corner blocks, on the
  four tall blocks and just north of the tower. Asked again with the zones drawn, they rate the four corner endings the
  best on the map.
- **What counts in the zone:** each zone now counts only its own heights. In the centre, a player hiding in the tower's
  rooms anywhere up to 110 metres used to count as standing in the zone; now only the street, the court below and the deck
  do. On the four tall blocks only the roof counts, so a squad at the foot of the block or one player in an office below
  can no longer stop the roof's holder.
- **Settling in:** a player counts in the zone after 2 seconds inside, so running through it or being thrown over it by a
  pad no longer freezes the other squad's meter.
- **Next:** the four tall roofs get structures to fight round and a second way up, the deck north of the tower gets cover
  and walls to run, and decks go across the ring road. That is a bake, version 148.
