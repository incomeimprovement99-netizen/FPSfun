# 1 October 2026

**The central tower has floors to fight on all the way up.** A staircase now runs up the middle of the new map's tower
from its lobby to its top floor, with a door onto every floor on the way. Between 14 and 35 m there are eight new
floors, each laid out differently: two open floors with crates and furniture for cover, two of rooms, two split into big
halls joined by wide gates, and two mazes of small rooms. Every floor is closed in by the tower's walls and windows, so
you can fight your way up without being shot from outside.

**The range's armory.** The back wall of the range, behind where you spawn and either side of the doors to the Runs,
is now the armory: all ten guns and all ten hacks out to try. Each one turns as a hologram over its own open case,
with a card over it in the style of the hack cards you inspect, and a screen on the wall behind it.

- The guns stand left of the middle door (THE CHAIN), the hacks right of it, the mobility hacks first. Three of each
  stand further out, past the Run doors.
- A gun's screen gives its name and class, what it is good at, and its damage, headshot damage, fire rate, magazine,
  reload and time to kill. Each number is shown at the level you carry the gun at and, in green, one level up. Under
  that it says what level 5 adds in all, and the strip along the bottom says how to take and fuse it.
- A hack's screen says what it does, its key, which slot it goes in and whether that slot holds it. It shows the
  cooldown at each level with yours lit, and says how to take, fuse and swap it.
- Walk up to a stand and look at it: the prompt says what E does. E takes the gun, replacing the one in your hand
  when both slots are full, or puts the hack into its slot.
- Press E again at the same stand to fuse it a level up, as a second copy does in a match: to level 5 for a gun, 4 for
  a hack. The gun's hologram changes skin with its level, as the gun in your hands does.
- The old racks by ammo type, and the ammo crates and clutter along that wall, are gone from the SpeedKills range.

**Loot on every floor of the tower.** Every floor of the new map's tower now has loot of its own, about eight items a
floor each match, so climbing it is worth the fight. Before, almost all of the middle's loot landed on the ground floors
and in the court under the base.

**The soldier's swap is ready for the new in-place swap.** The guns side is changing your own swap so the gun stays in
your hands and dissolves from the outside in while the next one builds from the middle out, the hands opening around
it. The soldier everyone else sees now does the same the moment that lands: the gun stays up, the hands open and turn
toward it, and close on the new gun as it appears. Until then the soldier's swap stays as it is. Also checked between
two real game pages: when a friend reloads from empty you see the full reload, and with rounds left the quicker one.

**BOOG carried more cleanly on the run.** Today's frame-by-frame check of the soldier found 16% fewer problem frames
than yesterday's. The one thing that had got worse, BOOG's stock touching the chest while running and sprinting, is
now fixed: the problem frames on the run dropped from 14 to 6 and on the sprint from 20 to 4. The gun's carry is now
checked through the whole stride instead of at one moment of it. BOOG's long stock still tucks into the arm in a jump,
mostly out of sight, and that is still on the list.

**The killcam no longer freezes as it starts.** In a battle royale, the moment the killcam began after you were
eliminated, the game stopped for about half a second while it rebuilt every player in the match for the replay. Now
the players come into the replay one at a time over its first moments, you first and then those nearest your killer,
and the game keeps running smoothly while they do.

**Into the tower from the base's roof.** The roof of the base round the new map's tower now leads straight onto the
tower's terrace and in through the doorways on its north and south sides, so there is a second way up into the tower
besides its staircase.

**Classic bot matches: the bot no longer gets stuck by the middle lane's wall.** In the classic game's warehouse, a
bot that came down the inside of the middle lane's wall could walk into the box at its end and stay there for the
whole match. Now it backs out further each time it finds itself stuck in the same spot, and gets round to you.

**A second bug hunt.** Every mode was played again on today's build, with the new armory and tower floors in. It
turned up one small thing: picking up a second gun downloaded your hands' pickup animation again. Now it is
downloaded once.

**No more black walls or flickering floors in the tower.** Inside the new map's tower, the walls round its corners were
pitch black, and the floor at their feet flickered in a jagged pattern as you moved. The walls are now plaster inside,
lit like the rest of the room. The flicker came from two surfaces drawn in exactly the same place. The map is now
checked for that all over the base and the tower, which also cleared the same flicker from the base's ground floor and
the basement under it.

**A smoother ride in the drop ship.** About nine seconds into the battle royale's ride the picture used to catch for
a quarter of a second, the first time the ship flew over the other players and the loot. They are now made ready
while the match loads, and the first moment aboard is smoother too.

**The magazine phases round its middle.** Reloading the USSO or BOOG, the old magazine now dissolves from its edges in
until only a few glowing cells are left at its middle, and the new one builds from its middle out, the way the guns
themselves phase when you swap.

**BOOG's left hand.** The ring finger on BOOG's left hand was broken in every frame you held it. It is whole again, and
the left wrist bends less while it points at the magazine on a reload.

**The heavy pistol is APUHTHEE.** The pistol that came in for PULSAR had a working name until now. It is APUHTHEE
everywhere: in your loadouts, the Loadouts tab, the guides and the docs. At that point nine of the ten guns carried your
own names; the tenth, the Energy LMG, is CHOOCH since the next change.

**Glass lifts up to the High City islands.** From the walkway ring round the new map's middle, a footbridge now crosses
the street to a glass lift beside each of the four tall islands. Step in, press interact facing the glowing rope, and
it carries you up the island's side in about two seconds and puts you on a landing that leads onto the roof. It brings
you back down the same way. Each island's rope glows in its own colour.

**The Well.** The new map's south-east block now has a deep light-well where its office building stood: a raised plaza
at street level round the opening, under a big lit sign, and three galleries of shops going down round the well to
10 m below the street. A glowing yellow rope runs up the middle. Interact facing it at the bottom to be carried up and
over the railing onto the street, or lean on the railing, look down and interact to ride to the bottom. Stairs between
the galleries and a tunnel from the metro station come next.

**Stairs down the Well.** A stairwell in the street on the Well's north side now leads down to the first gallery, and a
stair from each gallery to the next takes you all the way to the bottom, 10 m down, crossing every gallery of shops on
the way. The rope up the middle is still there for the quick way.

**The Energy LMG is CHOOCH.** NOVA is renamed everywhere: your loadouts, the Loadouts tab, the guides and the docs.
All ten guns now carry your own names.

**A tunnel from the metro to the Well.** A lit corridor now runs from the east end of the new map's underground station
to the bottom of the Well, so you can come up from the platform through the Well's galleries to the street, or go down
the Well to catch the metro.

**The soldier others see matches your new reload and swap.** When an enemy reloads, their magazine now melts away
from the outside in and the new one grows from its middle, as in your own hands. Their swap is the new in-place one:
the gun dissolves from its ends while their hands cup around it, and the next gun grows from its middle as the hands
close on it. A reload with rounds left skips the charging handle or bolt for them too.

**The Well is closed in.** From the Well's galleries you could see out through gaps between the shops to an empty
sky-coloured void under the street. A wall now stands behind all of it, with the tunnel's doorway left open, so every
look from a gallery meets a wall or a shop.

**Enemies punch the way you do.** When another player melees, their soldier now keeps the gun in the right hand,
pulls the left hand back by the chest and punches out with a fist, then puts the hand back on the gun, the same as in
your own view. It used to jab with the whole gun. With that, the soldier others see matches your first person in the
reload, the swap and the melee.

**A shared plan for the other eight guns.** Both agents now work from one plan for the guns after the USSO and BOOG
(docs/PLAN_THE_EIGHT_GUNS.md): which gun comes first and why, what each one needs in your own view and in what others
see, what both must agree before starting, the lessons from the first two guns, and the questions only you can answer.
It starts once you sign off the first person's USSO and BOOG.

**Faster frames, most of all on High.** A look at where each frame's time goes found the game doing work nobody
sees: on High it drew the city's shadows twice a frame, every bot in the match was drawn every frame even when it was
behind you or across the map, each bot's skeleton was worked out four times over, and the whole city recalculated
where its unmoving buildings stand sixty and more times a second. All of that is gone. On a fast PC, High in the
middle of a battle royale went from about 100 to about 150 frames a second and Balanced from about 180 to about 280;
on a slower processor High roughly doubled. High now runs about as fast as Balanced did, so if you were on Balanced
for the frames, High is worth a try.

**Fire escapes onto the rooms buildings' roofs.** The three corner buildings with rooms inside, the MOTEL one and its
twins, each have a fire escape up an outside wall. Its bottom stair hangs over the street like a real drop stair: jump
up to it, climb three flights and step over the low wall onto the roof, which you could not reach before.

**The USSO and BOOG are held like Apex's R-99 and Sentinel.** Both guns now lie flat across the bottom right of the
screen and point where you are looking, the way the R-99 and Sentinel do in Apex and the Harpy and Protocol V in Hyper
Scape. Before, they were angled up and off to the left. The USSO's left arm no longer lies across the gun: only the hand
and the end of the forearm show, coming up from the bottom of the screen. Reloading now brings the magazine into plain
view, inspecting looks as it did before, and the melee punch no longer drags a sleeve across the screen.

**Walled yards on those roofs.** At the top of each fire escape, the roof now has a walled yard of slatted concrete,
cover on an open roof the High City decks look down on.

**A plan for how other players will see the eight new guns.** Before building any of them, the soldier you see on
other players was checked holding each of the eight as it does today, frame by frame. They are all held only roughly
for now, hands through the gun, so each gets a proper fit. The plan says what each gun needs, what is likely to go
wrong on it, and how that gets caught. Two problems turned up while writing it. When CHOOCH overheats, other players
see the start of a reload, which will be replaced by the vent. And if you had picked STRYDER or HAEFY in a loadout,
the game would have quietly swapped it for the default when those guns change; it will keep your pick instead.

**1v1s in the middle of the Neon City.** SpeedKills' 1v1, with a friend or against bots, now plays on THE CENTRE:
the battle royale city's own middle, its tall tower, the podium round its foot and the circular Sky Ring, inside a
glowing circle you cannot leave. The capture circle is on the ring road beside the tower. The old 1v1 map, NEON
BLOCK, is still in the Map box.

**The superglide meter works again.** The bar under the crosshair now has a needle that runs across it while you
mantle: jump when it is in the green, crouch right after. After a good superglide you see both your JUMP and your
CROUCH marks; before, the crouch's label covered the jump's.
