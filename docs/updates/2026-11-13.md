**The light, measured (nothing in the game changed yet).**

- **What was found:** the city is lit from 41 degrees up at every hour. The golden hour's low sun is only painted in the sky. This morning's analysis blamed the low sun for the black shade, and that was wrong.
- **What does lift the shade:** the sky fill. Raised from 0.75 to 1.7 it takes the near-black share of six test pictures from 21% to 13%, with the golden look kept. A softer tone mapping (AgX) reaches 11%, and 9% with the fill, but it greys the neon.
- **A fault:** press F8 twice in a match and the golden hour comes back darker, lit from the low painted sun (the near black goes from 21% to 27%).
- **For the owner:** a page of the options to pick from. Nothing changes until then.
- **A new tool:** tools/neon-darkshare.cjs counts the dark pixels of any folder of pictures, so a change to the light can be judged by a number.
