# 2 October 2026

**A coloured beam over each corner block.** A tall beam of light now rises over each corner block in its own colour:
pink over MOTEL HILL, red over NOODLE ROW, green over MARKET and teal up the middle of THE WELL. You can see where each
block is, and where its loot is, from anywhere on the map.

**Street food on NOODLE ROW.** The north-east block now has four street food stalls by its streets, each with two
paper lanterns at its front. Their counters are cover.

**Crates on MARKET.** The south-west block now has stacks of crates by its streets, cover to fight from.

**Buildings to walk into on the curved streets.** On each corner block one of the buildings along the curved
streets is now a real building with rooms and stairs inside: walk in from the street and fight through its floors.

**Zip lines to the High City.** From the roof yards on MOTEL HILL and NOODLE ROW a lit zip line now runs up to the
nearest High City deck: ride it up from the roof, or down from the deck onto the roof.

**No more "the ring is closing" on the dropship.** SpeedKills has no ring, so the announcer no longer says one:
nothing is said on the ship, a decay wave after you land says "Decay incoming", and the capture zone opening says
so.

**The keys on screen are easier to read.** The keys on their white caps were smeared by a dark outline; they now
have a light one, and the list is a weight lighter.

**Faster mantles.** Climbing up onto a ledge is now 1.75 times as quick, so it no longer stalls you mid-run.
Superglides off the end of a mantle are a little easier to time than before.

**The guns sound like guns.** Every gun now fires with a real recorded shot from the animation pack we bought,
picked for the kind of gun it is: the pistol is a revolver, BIGANTLER a pump shotgun, CHOOCH a machine gun. The
squeaky whine over BOOG's and CHOOCH's shots is gone. The USSO and BOOG reload with the pack's own reload sounds,
every click landing where the hands are, and BOOG works its bolt after each shot. Reloading from an empty magazine
makes a sound now too (it was silent).

**SLAM lands with a boom.** Its landing squeaked; it is a heavy boom now.

**The tower's ground floor is open.** The big building round the tower's foot has lost its shop walls on the ground
floor: it is one open hall behind a row of pillars, open to the plaza on every side, with low cover you can see over.
The floors above stay walled.

**Your player card while a battle royale loads.** The loading screen now shows your card, the way Apex shows each
player's banner: your name, level and banner title, your soldier holding your gun, your wins, kills and the move
you have landed most, your two guns and two hacks, and the match's setup. Beside it, tips for the battle royale
turn over: how the decay closes in, the capture zone, fusing guns and hacks, the jump pads and lifts, and every move
you can make.

**Jump pads up the tower, and back down.** Four pads round the tower's base throw you onto its roof, and a pad on the
roof throws you on up to a lookout 49 m high on the tower's east side. A pad up there throws you back down onto the base's
roof. White beams mark the ways up and a red one the way down.

**Other players now see ANAKIN held properly.** The steady SMG is the first of the eight new guns fitted on the
soldier other players see. Before today their hands went through it. Now the right hand holds the top of its grip with
the trigger finger on the trigger, the left hand holds the flat underside of its front, and it is carried properly when
standing, aiming, sprinting, jumping and swapping. Its reload is the magazine alone for now; the charging handle comes
once its first-person reload is made. One thing is still off: its long stock runs along the inside of the soldier's
right forearm, which you can see from the side. That is next on the list, together with the same problem on BOOG.

**A sharper check of guns against the soldier's body.** The tool that measures whether a gun goes into the soldier
missed anything more than 4 cm deep. Fixed, it found ANAKIN going into the soldier's belly during a sprint jump, which is
now fixed, and a few deep spots on BOOG that are next on the list. The USSO came out clean.

**A better soldier on your card.** On the battle royale's loading card your soldier now faces you, standing ready
with the USSO (or BOOG) in his hands and both boots flat on the ground; before, the gun hung at his side and he stood
on his heels. Your two hacks are big tiles now, each with its key, what it does and its level.

**Open windows in the tower.** The tower's floors and the first and second floors of the big building round its foot now
have open windows, no glass: regular-sized ones you can shoot through, jump in through or leap out of. From the roof
round the tower's foot, a double jump and a grab get you into the lowest tower floor.

**Changing the time of day mid-match no longer freezes the game.** The first switch between the neon night, golden
hour and hazy day used to hold the screen for about three seconds; it is instant now.
