# 2 October 2026

**A coloured beam over each corner block.** A tall beam of light now rises over each corner block in its own colour:
pink over MOTEL HILL, red over NOODLE ROW, green over MARKET and teal up the middle of THE WELL. You can see where each
block is, and where its loot is, from anywhere on the map.

**Street food on NOODLE ROW.** The north-east block now has four street food stalls by its streets, each with two
paper lanterns at its front. Their counters are cover.

**Crates on MARKET.** The south-west block now has stacks of crates by its streets, cover to fight from.

**Buildings to walk into on the curved streets.** On each corner block one of the buildings along the curved
streets is now a real building with rooms and stairs inside: walk in from the street and fight through its floors.

**Zip lines to the High City.** From the roof yards on MOTEL HILL and NOODLE ROW a lit zip line now runs up to the
nearest High City deck: ride it up from the roof, or down from the deck onto the roof.
