## Finishers (Milestone 181)

The owner dropped the buy station ("no point") and kept finishers and voice. A knocked enemy in front of
you and the melee key: you crouch over them and throw a hook and a cross, they go back on the last blow,
and at the end they are dead and your shield is full. A hit on you breaks it off.

Two faults turned up on the way, both caught by the kind of check the audit asked for:

- **The blow could miss.** The kill first went out as a melee ray from your eye, and the proof run (the
  shield reward and the break-off taken out to watch their checks fail) showed a finisher that played to
  the end on a bot that was still alive: the ray had passed over a figure on the floor. It reaches the
  figure the way a bullet's hit does now.
- **It looked wrong.** The first snapshot had the finisher standing and punching a metre over the knocked
  head, and the knocked figure flat on its back from the first frame. The finisher crouches, and the
  knockback waits for the last blow.

## Voice and a drop theme (Milestone 182)

The game talks now, in the browser's own speech, in two voices:

- **Yours:** "Enemy knocked!", "I'm down!", "Enemy spotted!" on a ping.
- **The match's:** the drop, the ring closing, the final ring, three and two squads left, the end.

Free recorded voice lines are scarce and mismatched, and a set voice reading short lines is what an
announcer is. The drop theme is a CC0 track from OpenGameArt that plays from boarding to landing. Settings
has a slider for each, and all the way down is off.

## A flaky check that was hidden

Running the bot squads section alone, again and again for the finisher, showed a fourth flaky check.
"Each squad out of a fight keeps together" passed 40 of 40 in every full batch on record. Alone,
over eight runs, it measured anywhere from 13 of 40 to 40 of 40, and three runs came in under its 55%
limit. It fails the same way with this
work stashed, so it is not the finisher's. It goes on the bug hunt's list with the other three
(`docs/TEST_AUDIT.md`).
