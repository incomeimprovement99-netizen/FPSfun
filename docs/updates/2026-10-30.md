# 2026-10-30

**A dead lobby stays dead.** If your connection to the matchmaking server dropped for a while as you waited for a
friend, the game told you the lobby was over, but a friend using the old invite could still land in it later. Now it
really is closed.

**Firing range fixes.** A fusion level you tried out in the range no longer carries into 1v1s, bot matches or the
arena modes, can't be used on THE RUN's lent pistols, and stays on your gun after THE RUN or the tutorial. A shotgun no
longer clears several flick-drill targets with one shot, DEVINOS can now clear them at all, and the drill stops if you
leave. One shotgun shot on the manual's arrows turns one page, the spray wall shows the hip-fire pattern when you fire
from the hip, strafing dummies now sidestep instead of running on the spot, and the practice numbers start fresh after
a match. The dummies' shoot-back shield is the game's 50.

**Match ends.** The end-of-match screen in 1v1s, bot matches and the arena modes showed two summary cards and the result
on top of each other; now there's one card, under the result. A new round or rematch no longer starts with the last
round's walls and mines still up or your hacks still on cooldown, and a kill that landed in a last-moment trade no
longer counts toward the next match.

**Sound and captions.** Captions no longer fill up with your own footsteps and gunfire (pushing off the sounds of other
players near you), and your own landing no longer says "SOMEONE LANDED". Setting Effects or Hit sounds to 0 now really
silences them (their echo used to get through). You can now hear other players reload, and hear when someone else uses
REVEAL. Pings in the battle royale have their own sound instead of the hit-marker beep. When a match ends by someone
holding the capture zone, the announcer now says so instead of "last squad standing". "Get ready to drop" is said when
you actually see the ship, guests now hear "Squad wiped", and you can hear your friends moving around in the firing
range together.

**HUD and loot.** Picking up a gun in the middle of a weapon swap now puts it where the card said, with its own rounds.
The loot card shows a dropped gun's actual magazine, compares the CHOOCH sensibly, and no longer offers "HOLD: TAKE ALL"
when holding would take nothing. At larger HUD scales the loot card no longer covers the prompt or minimap, and the kill
feed no longer runs over your ammo count. The REVEALED warning goes away when you die or the match ends, and the Tab card
shows your real reserve ammo and exact cooldowns.

**Pings, sprays and emotes.** Pings land where you look even down in the sunken court or the station, the ping wheel's
markers no longer land 300 m away, and loot behind a wall isn't pinged. Double-tapping an enemy keeps tracking them, an
"enemy here" no longer wipes your "defending here", and a pinged enemy who turns invisible isn't tracked. Sprays on a
WALL hack disappear with the wall, ghosts can't spray, emotes don't play while you're shooting, and a squad mate's pings
disappear when they leave.

**Shade you can see into.** Walls, roads and tanks in shade are no longer pure black: you can see their surfaces and
whoever is standing against them, on Balanced and High (Competitive is unchanged). At night the city past the fence
glows half as bright, so the map itself is the brightest thing in view.

**The drop and the connection.** A dive that ends by climbing onto a roof now counts as landing, where your gun stayed away
and the trigger stayed locked until your next jump. Coming back from a won Gulag lands you on safe ground, not in a decayed
sector beside your mate. WALL, HEAL and MINE no longer work in a dive. If a friend lands first the fight starts for them,
even while you are still riding the ship. Bots no longer fall through the street at low frame rates. When a friend's
connection drops, the others keep seeing them while the host holds their seat, with no LOST messages. Players are drawn
closer to where they really are when they stop and start, and a figure no longer slides on past where someone stopped.

**The server.** A single malformed message could stop the whole game server, and nothing stopped anyone sending it; that is
closed. The server also can't be swamped by one client's messages, filled by one address making accounts (20 a day now) or
have its log grown without limit. A damaged accounts file is kept instead of being overwritten. Sign-ups in the last moment
before a restart are no longer lost. A release that fails to start now puts the previous one back by itself.

**Bots and the arena modes.** Your hits on someone who just used DASH now count. Bots stay inside THE CENTRE's wall in the modes,
stand still until FIGHT, respect crouching, and no longer get stuck in walls. A beginner no longer outruns Easy. A bot on decayed
ground now runs for safety instead of standing and dying. In Control, standing on the Sky Ring above a zone no longer holds it, you
won't respawn on top of an enemy, and everyone now sees the zone messages. A rematch starts you at your own start with a full
scoreboard, and a host left alone no longer racks up free wins. ARMOR now counts properly in every mode.

**Loading.** If the city fails to load you're told and sent back, not dropped on nothing after three minutes. A slow connection
keeps waiting as long as the city is still coming. Alt-Tabbing during the wait no longer breaks it. The Graphics setting no longer
reloads the page in the middle of a match.

**Enemy bodies, tidied after a review.** Fresh reviewers went through this week's soldier work and found things I had
missed. A soldier turning then walking no longer has its hips snap round. A respawned soldier no longer comes back
twisted. A body falling at an edge or on stairs lands properly: two faults there were mine, from yesterday. Your own
third-person death now falls the right way. And rounds no longer pass through a target running straight at you, up to 5 in
100 in the worst case.

**CHOOCH's head, and pistols on ledges.** A soldier jumping out of a sprint with CHOOCH held it right over its face, so
from the front you saw a drum where the head should be; it now holds it across the chest. And a pistol no longer hangs
off the hand during a climb: it is put away and comes back after.
