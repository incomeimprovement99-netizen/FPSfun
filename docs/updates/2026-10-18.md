# 2026-10-18

**The tutorial looks like the Neon City now.** Its rooms are built from the same pack of art as the battle royale's
city: lit tech walls, dark glowing floors, yellow ledges to jump onto, dark glass where you cannot run, neon signs and
lanterns. It loads in a few seconds, and while you play it the city downloads in the background, so the battle
royale after it starts sooner.

**A bigger start screen.** The Tutorial and the Firing Range now sit side by side as big boxes, with Battle Royale
under them in the middle, and blue and pink code rain falls behind it all. Under them a line says your settings stay
in this browser, with links to Settings and to the new Account tab, where you can sign in to keep them everywhere.

**THE RUN rebuilt.** The timed run behind the left-hand gate is about three times as long and built for how fast
you move now: out up one side and back down the other, through a jump, a slide, a climb, a double jump, a wall
run, a superglide, walkers to shoot, high ground and a final sprint. No ziplines. Best times start again on it.

**The old guided tour is gone.** The tutorial teaches everything it did and more, so the tour's button under More
has been taken out. Start from the start screen's Tutorial.

**The tutorial, fixed from the first playtest.** The wall run room can be passed now: room 4's ledge no longer traps
you above the door, and a jump from beside the lit wall catches it (follow the lit line on the floor). Holding E to
skip puts you in the next room. A green beam of light and a GO HERE marker show where to go in every room, the
see-through gaps in the walls are closed, and the ceiling is the Neon City's.

**The start screen, tidied again.** The code rain now covers more of the game behind it, the three boxes are a
little smaller with outlined words, and the SPEEDKILLS name runs across about half the screen.

**A smoother city, and the plan to finish it.** The city now draws most of its buildings in a few big batches instead of
hundreds of small pieces, which takes a lot of work off your processor: on a slow laptop it ran about a quarter faster
in our test. And the plan to finish the city is set: every outer district filled out properly (cover everywhere, THE
WORKS made into real machinery halls, THE TERRACES made into real terraces, THE STRIP lit up), then shops you can walk
into all round the centre.

**Clearer air, no glare.** The haze over the city is much thinner, so from the dropship and the rooftops you can see
the whole map, and the night is clearer too. Lamps and ceiling lights no longer blow out into big white glows.

**Hit numbers over heads, and REVEAL through walls.** Damage numbers now float above the enemy's head instead of
covering their body, and enemies found by REVEAL show as a red outline and shape through walls.

**The firing range is now a Neon City street.** It's a street at night: lit buildings on both sides, wet asphalt and
neon signs, with the gun and hack stands, the moving targets and the README screen set up in it. It still loads in a
moment. The old warehouse, its platforms, ladders and practice walls are gone: the tutorial teaches the moves now.

**The range's street has street lights.** The city's own lamps stand along both kerbs now, and the raised targets
stand on concrete plinths instead of black boxes.

**You can walk out of the tutorial again.** The last door shut on you as you walked through it and put you back
inside, with every door behind you shut too. A door now never shuts while you are standing in it.

**Wall runs and skydives you can read.** Other players running a wall now lean off it as they run, feet on the wall
and gun up, instead of floating beside it in a jump pose. In the battle royale's drop, a player gliding lies belly down
with arms out and one diving goes head first, so you can tell where a squad is heading; their gun comes back out when
they land.

**Hands that hold their guns.** ANAKIN's left hand now holds the gun from underneath, just ahead of the trigger, with
its fingers wrapped round the far side and its thumb along the near side. Before, it hung on the front corner with its
fingers and thumb pointing off into the air. Five more guns had a finger or thumb sticking out (REZ, CHOOCH, BIGANTLER,
the USSO and DEVINOS); each now rests on its gun.

**Empty hands like Apex's.** With nothing in your hands you no longer see two fists held out in front of you jabbing
the air as you run. As in Apex: standing or walking your hands are out of sight; sprinting, an open hand swings up
into the bottom of the screen with each step; a melee swings your right fist into the middle of the screen. Melee with
empty hands now shows the punch (it had shown nothing).

**Enemies read better.** A round on someone's shield makes them flash in the shield's colour, and the round that
breaks it bursts the shield off them in shards, with a cracked-shield marker on your crosshair. An enemy using ARMOR
glows gold, and your damage numbers on them are the real, reduced ones. You hear other players land, slide and
climb where they are, and when an enemy's REVEAL catches you, a red REVEALED warning tells you. The minimap no
longer turns upside down while you watch a squad mate or the killcam, and it marks north.
