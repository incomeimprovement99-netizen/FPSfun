# 2026-10-18

**The tutorial looks like the Neon City now.** Its rooms are built from the same pack of art as the battle royale's
city: lit tech walls, dark glowing floors, yellow ledges to jump onto, dark glass where you cannot run, neon signs and
lanterns. It loads in a few seconds, and while you play it the city downloads in the background, so the battle
royale after it starts sooner.

**A bigger start screen.** The Tutorial and the Firing Range now sit side by side as big boxes, with Battle Royale
under them in the middle, and blue and pink code rain falls behind it all. Under them a line says your settings stay
in this browser, with links to Settings and to the new Account tab, where you can sign in to keep them everywhere.

**THE RUN rebuilt.** The timed run behind the left-hand gate is about three times as long and built for how fast
you move now: out up one side and back down the other, through a jump, a slide, a climb, a double jump, a wall
run, a superglide, walkers to shoot, high ground and a final sprint. No ziplines. Best times start again on it.
