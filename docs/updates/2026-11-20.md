# 2026-11-20

**Map version 149: the new structures finished.**

- **Why:** the picture review found the centre's look had slipped from 6.4 to 6.0, because the decks, terraces and
  hoardings built over the last three batches for how the game plays looked like flat coloured placeholder panels.
- **What changed:** every one of those decks and terraces now has a proper floor on top, a painted metal edge, a riveted
  underside with girders, and a lit edge strip in its quarter's colour; the pillars are bare steel (one had a tree-bark
  texture); the run walls are lit and hung from a truss. Nothing a player walks on or collides with moved.
- **Also:** THE WORKS' painted containers no longer glow like lightboxes, and THE TERRACES' hedges are a deeper green.
- **Judged:** a fresh picture reviewer scored the centre 6.1, up from 6.0. The fixes read as fixed; the new complaint is
  that the streets under the decks are now dark, "a black cave" at night. Lights under the decks are next.
