# 2026-10-21

**PANDA held by its grip.** Your right hand now closes round PANDA's grip, the way ANAKIN's and BIGANTLER's do.

**Arms that look like arms.** On most guns your elbows now stay where a real elbow would, so your forearms no longer twist round as you aim, swap or inspect. REZ's was the worst: aiming in rolled the right forearm half way round.

**Tell a gun by its flash and its sound.** Enemies' muzzle flashes now match the gun: a small flicker from an SMG, a big blast from a shotgun or the sniper. Far-off gunshots keep each gun's own sound instead of every gun of a kind sounding the same.

**Your squad's shots are blue.** A teammate's tracers are cyan, the enemy's red, so your squad's fire past you no longer looks like fire at you.

**See where near misses come from.** When an enemy's shot flies close past you, a thin white arc round your crosshair now points to where it came from, the way a red arc does when you are hit.
