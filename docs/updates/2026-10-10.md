# 2026-10-10

**Two small 1v1 maps.** A 1v1 now opens in NEON YARD, a walled yard open to the sky, with crates, cool boxes and a
wall across each end; NEON HALL, a closed concrete hall with pillars and lamps overhead, is the other choice in the Map
box. Both are built from the same Neon City pieces as the big map, small and plain on purpose, and the same both ways
round, so neither spawn has the edge. THE CENTRE now hosts free-for-all, team deathmatch and Control.

**The tower, all the way up.** The big tower's top half was an empty shell. Now its stair carries on past the old top
floor all the way to the crown at 109 m, and there are three new floors on the way: a bright sky lobby at 60 m with
windows at eye height over the whole city, a rusty machine hall at 75 m, and a gold penthouse loft at 90 m, two storeys tall. Two gold express
ropes run non-stop from the floor under the old lid straight up to the penthouse in a few seconds, and from the sky lobby
or the machine hall you can grab them over their barriers and ride up or down. Low barriers round each rope's shaft keep anyone from
walking into it. The crown is still reached by its two pads from outside, and now by the stair too.

**Street pieces that make sense.** The tall poster boards along the streets were loose posters floating in the air with
nothing behind them. They are concrete walls now, with posters pasted on both sides, so what you hide behind is what you
see. The planters in the middle of the streets used to stop bullets with their leaves up to head height; now only the
planter box itself (up to a crouching body) is solid, and you can shoot through the plant above it.

**APUHTHEE and STRYDER are in your hands.** Both pistols are now held by the same arms as the other guns, out in front in
both hands, your left hand wrapped round your right on the grip, sitting where Apex holds its Wingman.
- **Their own sounds:** each now fires and reloads with its own recorded pistol sounds; APUHTHEE had a revolver's shots
  and STRYDER an SMG's, and neither had a reload sound of its own.
- **Their own kick:** APUHTHEE kicks up slow and heavy like a magnum, STRYDER light and fast.
