**Rooms on the street, a vault door standing open, roofs you can tell apart from the drop.** Six shops and market halls
along the street now have doors you walk through and a stair up to their roofs, and ten food kiosks stand among the street's
cover. THE VAULT's round door stands swung open on a black hinge with a spoke wheel, bars racked in the doorway, and lit gold
chests sit in the vault's halls, at THE WELL's bottom and on the station; the court and the base hall are furnished. The
roads are dry asphalt with kerbs and painted dashes rather than a mirror of the sky, and no car is parked across a door.
From the dropship LANTERN ALLEYS' roofs are terracotta, THE TERRACES' moss green and THE WORKS' banded yellow. THE CANYON has
six crossings with adverts over its long lanes, THE STACKS' court has a pod deck to climb, GLASS HEIGHTS' deck has frosted
lit rails and a lit lift cab, and past the fence a ring road runs round the city.
