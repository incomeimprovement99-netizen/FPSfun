# 2026-10-11

**Headshots from behind.** You can now see a soldier's head from behind and headshot it. The collar of its armour and suit
used to stand up behind the head and hide a third of it; it now sits below the head. A soldier crouched and aiming no
longer raises a shoulder up beside its head, and one aiming down no longer hunches its head down between its shoulders.
HAEFY is carried muzzle up across the shoulder until it is aimed, so its tube is no longer beside and behind the head.
And a shot at the head you can see now counts as a headshot: the invisible shapes a shot hits were rebuilt to follow the
body more closely (the head is the head's own shape, the arms are two pieces each, the chest bends on its own), and a
shot that just clips the neck or the shoulder on its way into the head is a headshot. Measured from 30 m behind and to
either side, in every pose with every gun and kit: every shot at a skull you can see takes the head, and nothing of the
soldier's kit hides the skull standing, walking, running, sprinting, crouched, jumping or aiming. Still in the way: HAEFY
aimed in (the tube is on the shoulder), BOOG aimed steeply down, and a little of the collar in a slide.

**Every gun kicks now.** Most guns barely moved in your hands when they fired, under a degree a shot. Each now kicks by
its kind: APUHTHEE, the magnum, snaps up hard; BIGANTLER shoves back like a pump shotgun; REZ and PANDA kick clearly; the
SMGs, CHOOCH and STRYDER tick with every round and climb a little through a burst. BOOG is as it was.
