# 2026-10-11

**Headshots from behind.** You can now see a soldier's head from behind and headshot it. The collar of its armour and suit
used to stand up behind the head and hide a third of it; it now sits below the head. A soldier crouched and aiming no
longer raises a shoulder up beside its head, and one aiming down no longer hunches its head down between its shoulders.
HAEFY is carried muzzle up across the shoulder until it is aimed, so its tube is no longer beside and behind the head.
And a shot at the head you can see now counts as a headshot: the invisible shapes a shot hits were rebuilt to follow the
body more closely (the head is the head's own shape, the arms are two pieces each, the chest bends on its own), and a
shot that just clips the neck or the shoulder on its way into the head is a headshot. Measured from 30 m behind and to
either side, in every pose with every gun and kit: every shot at a skull you can see takes the head, and nothing of the
soldier's kit hides the skull standing, walking, running, sprinting, crouched, jumping or aiming. Still in the way: HAEFY
aimed in (the tube is on the shoulder), BOOG aimed steeply down, and a little of the collar in a slide.

**Every gun kicks now.** Most guns barely moved in your hands when they fired, under a degree a shot. Each now kicks by
its kind: APUHTHEE, the magnum, snaps up hard; BIGANTLER shoves back like a pump shotgun; REZ and PANDA kick clearly; the
SMGs, CHOOCH and STRYDER tick with every round and climb a little through a burst. BOOG is as it was.

**The tests for playing with friends now test SpeedKills.** The game's automatic tests of playing over the internet
(a friend's figure staying smooth through a bad connection, what you look like to them), of the bots, the kill replay,
the jump pads and the firing range's tools were still running the old game, and now run SpeedKills. They turned up one
thing for the owner to decide: the range's spray wall and its yellow mark stand 40 m past the edge of SpeedKills'
range, where nobody can reach them.

**Leg shots land.** A shot at a soldier's leg used to miss more often than not: its legs were one invisible box
between them, and a leg out in a stride, or bent in a crouch, was outside it. Each leg now has its own thigh, calf and
boot, so a shot at a leg you can see hits it: about 7 in 10 land within a few centimetres of where you aimed, where 1 or
2 did, and fewer than 1 in 5 miss the leg altogether, where most did.

**Three fixes for playing with friends.** Moving the game's own tests of the battle royale with friends onto
SpeedKills turned up three faults, all fixed:
- **Out means out in solo.** In a solo match with a friend, or with friends split into squads against each other, a
  player taken out with only an opponent still standing was left a ghost, waiting for a squad mate who did not exist.
  Now they are out, and watch whoever is still standing.
- **Only SpeedKills' guns on the floor.** The middle of the city sometimes laid a gun that is not in SpeedKills at
  all (an old bow, the Defender, the Dragon LMG), wearing attachments the game does not have, and bots picked them up.
  Every gun you find now is one of the ten.
- **A cheat closed.** A friend's page could claim a hit with a made-up weapon name and no shot fired, for up to 250
  damage: one forged claim took a player out. The host now turns down any hit that does not name one of the ten guns,
  a melee swing, or a hack that does damage (SLAM and MINE, at no more than they can do).
