# 2026-10-20

**Inspecting a gun looks natural.** When you inspect the USSO, BOOG or ANAKIN, your left hand now comes up close to show
your hacks, as it does on every other gun. Before, as the inspect began and ended, it reached far out with the arm laid
straight across the screen.

**THE WORKS looks like works.** Its halls are in rusted metal now, and the big block hall in the corner has gas risers
glowing up through its atrium, electric cabinets, engines, tanks and pipes on its walls. The white shafts of light that
stood inside it are gone.

**THE TERRACES' rooms have things in them.** The little rooms in the terrace walls are lit, plastered and furnished:
a couch, a table and a chair, and a rack of goods.

**THE STRIP's billboards work.** The two screens either side of the tower's foot show their ads instead of solid
blue, and the big pyramid is a glass pyramid of lit windows instead of flat red.
