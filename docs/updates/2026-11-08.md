level.** THE VAULT's round door now opens onto a dark steel room with racks of gold bars lit by strips, and the bars are a

road, and the fence's red lines no longer streak across the sky. Down the Loop and the corner streets there is now

with zebra crossings, manholes and lit stairs up to the Sky Ring. GLASS HEIGHTS swapped its plain white blocks for frosted
screens in white planters with benches, and got shop signs and white decks. The kerbs are concrete instead of black bars
with blue dots, and THE TERRACES' entrance lost the last white street boxes.
