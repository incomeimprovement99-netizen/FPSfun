# 2026-10-24

**More light where it was dark.** THE TERMINAL's concourse and THE WORKS' halls are lit inside; THE STRIP's dance floor
is a glowing grid of magenta and cyan panes; and its pyramid's edges are lined in magenta, so it reads as a pyramid.

**The faults the reviewers kept naming, gone.** No more wireframe boxes round THE STRIP's and GLASS HEIGHTS' towers, no
white cones over the jump pads, no coloured poles over the corner blocks, no cars hanging on the lantern lines, and the
zip lines are thin dark cables. THE VAULT is gold now: gold doors, a gold floor, gold light along its walls. The centre's
streets have real cover where there were only plants you could shoot through.
