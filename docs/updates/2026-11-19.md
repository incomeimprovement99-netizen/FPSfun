**Batch 15 (map version 148): the ground the last fights are fought on.**

- **The four tall roofs:** when a match ends on one of the four tall blocks, its zone is that block's roof, and the
  reviewers called it the same bare standoff four times. Each roof now has a stair house and lit screens to fight round,
  placed so most ways up land out of sight of the zone's middle, and the west block has a new ledge stack up into its zone.
- **The deck north of the tower:** low concrete panels to fight behind and two walls to run along into the centre's zone.
  The walls are kept low on purpose: tall ones would have let a player watch the zone from a spot that does not count.
- **Decks across the ring road:** four bridges at 9 to 12 metres from the inner ring into the districts beyond, each with
  a way down. One is also a new way onto the south block's roof without a jump pad. They give routes over the open road;
  they do not hide the road itself.
- **Found by the bake and mended:** a check that walks each tall roof started from a point that the new pieces moved into
  a sealed corner of the north roof's crown; it now walks from the roof's main area.
- **Next:** round 4 of the layout review, and a picture round beside it to check the look has not slipped since the last
  one on version 144.
