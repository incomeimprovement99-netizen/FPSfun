# 2026-11-01

**A hole through the tower.** The tower's open floor at 26 m has a hole down into the maze floor under it, the first of
the tower's floors to break the stack: look down from one, up from the other. (Crates on the Sky Ring were tried a
second way and still blocked its walk, so they are out again.)

**The ground past the fence.** By day it is asphalt grey, not black, so the map no longer sits in a dark band.

**Your account keeps what you earned.** Playing on two devices can no longer wipe either one's progress: saves are merged, not
overwritten, and signing in keeps the best of both. Signing out sends your progress first, and a friend signing in on your browser
starts their own record.

**Fairer numbers.** Control no longer pays twenty times what a 1v1 does, a draw isn't a loss, the aim bot's scores stay off the boards,
and beginner matches now show in your stats.

**Fixes that decided matches.** A player who died mid-swap could turn invisible and unhittable to others for the rest of the match;
gone. When a host leaves, the new host no longer wins on the spot. The Gulag's countdown now holds your guns as well as the bot's.
Care package marks stay on screen, ghosts can ping, and a decay kill says the decay.

**Safer with strangers.** A host could run script in a guest's page, and a guest could one-shot anyone by claiming a hit with a
trick weapon name. Both are closed, and so are the ways one bad packet could fill the game with phantom figures or freeze a lobby.

**Hacks.** REVEAL against bots now ends, and shows how many it found. SLAM, LEAP and GRAPPLE won't burn their cooldown on a rope, and
WALL, HEAL and MINE land on the ground if you use them in the air. A mine or SLAM kill now counts as one on screen, INVISIBILITY ends
when you fire at any frame rate, and a ghost no longer gives itself away by looking around.

**Guns return to the hands.** After a throw, a climb or using something, a soldier's hands used to wait gripping nothing
while the gun stayed on its back, then the gun jumped across. Now it fades off the back and forms between the hands as they
arrive, the same way a weapon swap looks.

**Slides without the jump.** About 0.8 s into every slide, a soldier popped up to standing height for an instant and sank
back. That was a looping animation of mine. It's gone, on every gun, and the end of a climb no longer snaps upright either.

**A proper salute.** The salute emote's hand now reaches the brow; it used to stop at the collar.

**After you go down.** The killcam now fits the time before you're back, so you see the kill, even on the way to the Gulag, and a Gulag
loss has a replay. Spectating stays on the person you picked.

**Squads.** Restoring a mate no longer resets if your crosshair dips over their dropped guns, and you can bring a mate out of the Gulag
at their echo. Echoes stay reachable when the ground decays under them or they fall past the city's edge.

**Loot and the end of a match.** When a sector decays, what lay on it now falls to the ground instead of hanging in the air. Care package
items land on clear ground. Stepping off the capture zone keeps your progress on screen.

**Friends and voice.** A lobby stays open when a friend leaves, so the invite keeps working, and pasting a whole invite link into the code
box now works. Voice reconnects after a dropped connection, a muted friend stays muted between matches, and a call that arrives early
is tried again.

**New players.** Rounds and gun rules now work in matches alone. Phones are warned on the first screen. And a lot of old words in the menus
and manual now match the game as it is.

**Smoother soldiers.** A frame-by-frame scan of every change of stance found more one-frame jumps: a soldier going into a
slide threw its gun up 40 cm at once, every landing from a run dropped the body 25 cm in a frame, and aiming could snap the
gun. All of them now move over a few frames.
