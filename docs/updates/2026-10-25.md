# 2026-10-25

**The ways up are marked.** Each fire escape and each stair onto the Sky Ring and the base has a glowing doorway frame
at its foot in its area's colour, so you can see from the street where to climb.

**New ping and emote wheels.** Holding the ping or emote key now opens a ring of slices like Apex's, each ping
with its own picture, the one you point at lit up and named in the middle.

**Better with a controller and on smaller screens.** Controller players no longer see keyboard keys they cannot
press ("T: NEXT ITEM", "HOLD TAB"), and on a narrow screen the loot card and the carry card no longer cover the
keys list.

**THE WORKS' halls look like works.** Steel gantry rails run down each hall under the roof, and more engines stand on
the floors between the columns.

**The districts glow at their edges.** THE TERRACES' steps, THE CANYON's walkways, THE WORKS' halls and the capsule
hotel's roof are traced in their districts' neon colours, so at night each district reads as its own place instead of
going black.

**Easier to read the city.** Jump pads have a short glowing column over their rings so you can spot them from across a
street, each walk-in building has a lit gold doorframe, THE TERMINAL's halls have metro map boards, and at night the
lamps throw wider, softer pools, including under the Sky Ring. The 1v1 bot on THE CENTRE no longer gets stuck on two
street boxes north of the tower.
