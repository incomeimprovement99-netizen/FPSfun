# 2026-10-29

**Spectating shows the loot.** When you're out and watching a teammate, you now see the guns and hack cores around
them; before, anything more than 70 m from where you died wasn't drawn. And bots that pick up a better gun now drop
their old one, like players do, instead of making it vanish.

**Shooting fixes.** In a match with friends, switching to another gun right after a sniper headshot could make your
next hits not count for up to a second; they count now. Bots fire at the same rate whatever the host's frame rate (they
were firing slower on 60 Hz screens). And picking up a copy of the gun you're reloading now lets you fire it straight
away.

**Third-person aim.** In third person, your shots could miss an enemy right on your crosshair if there was open ground
behind them: the shot aimed at the wall far behind and passed beside them. They now land where the crosshair is.

**No more reload skip.** Dropping an empty gun and picking it straight back up used to give it back fully loaded and
ready to fire at once. A dropped gun now keeps however many rounds it had, and a gun you pick up into your hands takes
its normal time to come up.

**Switching modes while waiting for a friend.** If you had made an invite for one mode and then picked another mode
and pressed With friends, the screen showed the new mode but your friend still joined the old one. The waiting invite
now switches to the new mode.

**Dropped items stay on the stairs.** The earlier fix that drops mid-air items to the floor could also make a gun
dropped on a staircase fall through a gap to the floor below. It now rests where you would stand.

**Battle royale fixes.** Care packages no longer drop through roofs or into the tower; they land in the open. Bot
squads no longer sometimes land on top of each other at the tower. And a teammate with a slow computer (or the game in
a background tab) now takes the same damage from the decaying sectors as everyone else.

**Movement fixes.** Stepping up onto something with a low ceiling above it could throw you sideways, sometimes
metres; now it just stops you like a wall (there were a few hundred such spots, many in the tower). DASH now keeps your
speed as it was meant to, instead of stopping you dead. Jumping out of the dropship no longer uses up your double jump,
ziplines give your double jump back, and a wall run no longer carries on through a mantle or a zipline ride.

**No more slipping through walls on a hitch.** A stutter while moving fast could carry you straight through a thin
wall. Every fast move is now checked along the way.

**Bots step up kerbs instead of popping.** A bot (and your own soldier in third person) running onto a kerb or up stairs
jumped up the whole step in one frame. Its body now rises onto the step over a few frames, the way your own view
already did, with its feet on the step straight away. And a soldier standing still when it respawns no longer has its
legs stretched back toward where it was for a moment.

**Hack fixes.** DASH onto a low block now puts you on top of it instead of throwing you sideways out of it. A DASH that
can't go (a wall right in front of you) no longer breaks your invisibility. SLAM and MINE no longer hit through walls
or floors, a SLAM stops if you die mid-air, and a mine no longer goes off at once when someone walks back past it.

**THE VAULT in gold, a roof on THE STRIP's club.** THE VAULT's crates and racks are gold metal now, on a gold marble
floor, so its best-loot floor finally looks like a vault. The metro's front hall has the same concrete floor as its
concourse. THE STRIP's club has a dark roof with magenta light rigs under it, in place of the open upside-down pyramid
whose edges looked like loose strips from inside and a tilted slab from the street. The red edge
fence's lines fade out toward its top, so from a roof beside it they no longer streak across the sky. THE TERRACES' hedges
are green now instead of black slabs, and THE TERMINAL has a lit departures board and clock over its front arches.

**LEAP and invisibility.** After a LEAP you can now shoot while you glide (it used to lock your gun as if you were
skydiving from the ship, and in a battle royale the announcer said "Get ready to drop"), and the glide ends after a
few seconds as it should. In team deathmatch, Control and free-for-all, bots now lose track of you while you're
invisible, as they already did in battle royale. Other players' mines no longer stay on your screen after they've
gone off.

**Hacks in Arena, Bots.** Playing "Arena, Bots" on your own, SLAM, MINE, ARMOR and invisibility did nothing at all.
They all work there now, and hacks wait for the round to start like everywhere else.
