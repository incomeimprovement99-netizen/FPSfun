# 24 September 2026

Milestone 168 went live: hair on everybody, twenty distinct outlines, the clothes covering the body, and
the free-asset hunt written up. The three e2e batches passed except three checks in two sections known to
flake (bot tiers, and host migration in the battle royale), and both sections passed when run on their own.

Then the next ranked step: let a player pick the body. It found four faults on the way, all in snapshots.

**Every head was too narrow.** The body is narrowed towards the shoulders so the free cloth, cut for a
slimmer body, closes on it. The narrowing never eased back out, so the head was 29% narrower than the skull
the hair and hood were made for. It eases out now, above the shoulder line measured off each body: 1.50 m
on the male, 1.45 on the female.

**One figure in every lineup had a black face.** Each figure painted its body in its operator's shell
colour, a leftover from the grey mannequin. On a textured body that is a multiply over the skin, and an
operator with a near-black shell got a black, glossy face that followed the shape of the head. Only the
untextured mannequin is painted now, and the eyebrows take the hair's colour.

**The back of every shoulder showed through the sleeve.** Painting the body magenta for one shot showed it
was body, not cloth. Measured around the upper-arm bone, the body is 101 to 104 mm out through the deltoid
and the sleeves are cut for 60 to 69. The upper arm is brought in around its own bone to 0.8, eased back to
nothing at the elbow so the hand does not move, and a check measures the arm against every sleeve.

**Some skin still showed at the seams**: the armhole, the back of the knee, the ankle. Every piece was cut
against a different outline and no single shape closes them all. The body now wears a dark undersuit where
the clothes always cover it, as a vertex colour over the skin, which is how a seam gets hidden in games that
dress one body in many outfits. Face, neck, forearms and hands stay skin.

With the fit sound, the pick itself was small. Body is a row in the Loadouts tab; Build, which had done
nothing since our own garments came off, is the width of the chest, body and cloth together. The four "(F)"
outfits were the same clothes on the other body, so they went, and a stored one migrates. Hair follows the
body: the peasant on the lighter body had turned up with a beard.
