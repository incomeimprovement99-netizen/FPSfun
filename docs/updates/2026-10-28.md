# 2026-10-28

**Steadier matches with friends.** If the host leaves while a squad is holding the capture zone, the new host
carries on the meter instead of starting it over. And if your connection to the host drops for a moment, what you do
in those seconds (the guns you drop, a ping, a Gulag result) now reaches the match once you are back, instead of being
lost.

**The server is harder to knock over.** One malformed message to the matchmaking server could crash the whole game
server for everyone until it restarted; it now shrugs those off. One computer can no longer fill the matchmaking
server's connections, and accounts are better protected against password guessing.

**Settings and account fixes.** The controller list in Settings no longer repeats itself each time you change a
button or preset, and the Range preset's D-pad down shows its action. When a sync fails, the Account tab now says why
instead of always telling you to sign in again. And if your results aren't reaching the online boards (your name
belongs to an account you're signed out of), the Stats tab says so.

**Leaderboards protected from spam.** One computer can no longer fill a leaderboard with made-up names and push
everyone's real results off it.

**Slides you can shoot from.** A sliding enemy is drawn sat up with the gun at the eye, pointing where they look, the
way Apex shows it. Before, the slide laid the soldier on its back with the gun pointing 25 to 55 degrees off. Every gun
checked: no deeper in the soldier than standing aimed in.

**Small match fixes.** Winning the Gulag no longer drops you into a part of the city that is decaying. A bot that
never found a gun now gets a pistol instead of wandering around harmlessly all match. The gentlest bots no longer hunt
you down, the tutorial's first sign shows your own skip button, and you are no longer told you are taking decay damage
while you are dead and waiting to be brought back.

**Sound and voice fixes.** If you pressed push-to-talk near the end of a match and allowed the microphone after it
ended, the game could keep your microphone on afterwards; it now lets it go straight away. While spectating a squad
mate you now hear the footsteps around them, not around your own body. A few sounds (the care package horn, SLAM, the
zipline) no longer cut off with a click, the announcer no longer talks about the last match in the range, and
captions only show sounds that actually played.

**Voice chat reconnects.** If a friend's game wasn't ready to take your voice call (they had just joined, for
example), the game now tries again a few times instead of leaving them unable to hear you.

**Each tower floor its own, and the districts' asks.** The tower's floors are furnished differently now, a lobby,
offices, a store, a workshop, a server room, a lounge and a lab, so you can tell where you are inside it. The sunken
court's floor is calmer, and the corner blocks' roofs are traced in their colours. At night the ground past the fence has
lit roads, so the map no longer sits on a black table. THE CANYON has lit signs hung across its street, LANTERN ALLEYS'
way in from the ring road is all shophouses (the silver capsule buildings there made it look like THE STACKS), and THE
WORKS' halls are lined in teal from inside.

**A metro that looks like one, a vault with a steel ceiling, doors where the doors are.** The metro station's concourse
has a concrete floor now in place of the pink marble that made it look like a hotel corridor, the floor beside the
sunken court matches the court's, and THE VAULT has a dark steel ceiling over its gold walls instead of white office
plaster. The lit frames of the four walk-in buildings stood in front of blank wall, and two of the buildings had their
backs to the street: each now faces the street with its real door, and the frame stands round that door. The glowing
columns over the jump pads are slimmer, so one beside a wall no longer looks stuck into it. THE STACKS, LANTERN ALLEYS
and THE WORKS get more cover on their open ground, as do GLASS HEIGHTS' deck and THE TERRACES' tiers, and LANTERN
ALLEYS' sign is no longer hidden behind its lantern mast.

**The game loads with site data blocked.** If your browser blocks websites from saving data, the game used to sit on
its loading screen forever. It loads and plays now; your settings and progress just aren't kept after you close it.

**Team deathmatch, Control and free-for-all with friends.** If the host left a split-teams match, the new host's shots
went straight through the enemy bots; they hit now. A friend who lost their connection and never came back no longer
stays on the scoreboard (or wins a free-for-all on the clock), their frozen character no longer holds Control zones or
soaks up bot fire while their spot is kept, and when everyone on one side has left, the match ends and the side still
there wins instead of playing on against nobody.

**Enemies step when they turn.** A soldier standing still and turning to aim used to spin on the spot with both feet
sliding round on the floor. Now its feet stay planted while it turns, and once it has turned far enough it steps round,
one foot and then the other. Standing and crouched.

**Starting a team match early, and spawns.** If a host started a team deathmatch or Control before every spot was
filled, the bots were still set up for the full lobby, so the teams came out uneven; they're balanced for whoever is
actually in now, and the scoreboard no longer lists empty spots. In Control's five-a-side, one of your bots used to
start inside you; everyone gets their own starting point now.

**Friendly fire.** The "Friendly fire on" rule now actually works in team deathmatch and Control: your bullets,
rockets and hacks can hurt your teammates when it's on. And it no longer sneaks into battle royale: if you had turned it
on for a team match, a battle royale you hosted afterwards quietly let your squad hurt each other.

**Crouched enemies walk, not glide.** A soldier crouch-walking moved its legs at about a quarter of the pace it needed,
so it glided along with its feet sliding under it. Its steps now cover the ground it crosses, and the same for a slow
walk.

**Holding E to grab everything.** If one of your hacks was already at its top level and a core of it lay at your
feet, holding E kept picking it up and dropping it again forever, and never got to anything else. It now leaves that
core alone.

**Loot fixes.** A gun or hack you swap out in mid-air now drops to the floor instead of hanging in the air out of
reach. And if your connection drops and you get back in, your game catches up on everything that happened to the
loot while you were gone: items dropped (including a teammate's echo, so you can restore them), items picked up, care
packages.

**Strafing enemies run sideways.** A soldier strafing straight left or right had its legs turned only part of the way,
so its feet slid sideways along the floor at every step. Its legs now point the way it is going while its chest and
gun stay on you.

**Enemies at an edge stand on it.** The game lets a player stand with up to 40 cm of their body out past a ledge, a
step or a roof's edge, and their soldier was drawn standing in the air there, feet over the drop. Standing still, its
feet now go back onto the edge and its legs angle to them, like someone leaning out to look down.

**Bots step up kerbs instead of popping.** A bot (and your own soldier in third person) running onto a kerb or up stairs
jumped up the whole step in one frame. Its body now rises onto the step over a few frames, the way your own view
already did, with its feet on the step straight away.
