# 2026-10-19

**A HUD that looks finished.** Everything on screen now stands on dark see-through plates with cut corners,
like Hyper Scape's and Apex's. Your shield is slanted cells over a lit health bar that shows the health you just
lost in red for a moment. Each hack has its own picture, with the cooldown sweeping round it. Each gun shows its
own outline, drawn from the gun itself. The compass, the minimap, the counts and the kill feed got their frames too.

**No stutter as the ship flies over the bots.** The bots' guns were getting their graphics ready on the moment you
first saw them from the ship; now that happens in the background as soon as each bot is dressed.

**Sights you can't see through.** PANDA's scope was hollow: aiming down it, its tube was a see-through ring, and the
front of it was too. ANAKIN's sight showed through the base that holds it up. The sights are solid now on every gun,
in your hands and in an enemy's.

**You can see the fire coming at you.** Rounds shot at you now have a small glowing head, so incoming fire reads even
when it is coming straight at you (before, a bullet heading for you was a speck). Your own shots stay dim. An enemy's
muzzle flash is brighter and lasts a moment longer, with a soft glow round it, so you can spot who is shooting at range.
And enemies always hold their real guns: some could hold a plain grey stand-in for a whole match.

**Making a match after an update.** If you had the game open when it was updated, making a match could hang on
"Making a match..." for good. It now works, and if the game ever cannot load that part it tells you to reload.

**Skydivers land feet first.** In the battle royale's drop, other players now swing upright for their last moments
before the ground, feet first, instead of lying flat on the air until they touch down.

**You can see a grapple.** A player using GRAPPLE now throws their arm up at the spot it pulls them to, instead of
flying along the line in a jump pose.
