**Batch 8 (map version 141): THE VAULT's strongroom is a real room, the High City can be climbed, GLASS HEIGHTS has shops at street level, and the faults round 21 named are fixed.** The strongroom behind THE VAULT's round door now runs from floor to ceiling in dark steel trimmed gold, with five ways in instead of one: the door, a side door from each of the two halls beside it, and a drop into it from each hall on the floor above. Three of the four High City blocks have a stack of ledges up their outer face, 3 m apart from the street to the roof, so you can climb them. The base's roof has stair houses, chest-high walls and crates, and the Sky Ring's brick underside is lit. The streets round the centre have low barriers every 8 to 10 m, so the long lines down them are broken up. GLASS HEIGHTS' blank grey street storey is now seven shops with glass fronts, lit rooms and canopies, and its lift is one clear glass tube. THE WORKS' scattered crate piles are gone: its yard is a pipe rack and a crane over the containers, on concrete slabs with drains. LANTERN ALLEYS has a lantern pavilion round the mast in its court; THE TERRACES' rooms are plaster and timber; THE STACKS' planters are all pink. The city past the fence is darker by day and dimmer by night, so the play area is the brightest part of the view, and each district's own night colour is twice as strong. Every glass lift's top and zip line's end now hangs from a steel post, THE LOBBY's sign box in the atrium hangs from four rods, the NIGHT MARKET banner hangs above the walkway's fence instead of behind its posts, THE STRIP's crown is a dark cap with a magenta ring instead of a glowing disc, and the tiled ceilings that read as creased are plain plaster.

**Batch 9 (map version 142): the plaza is a paved square, the High City blocks each have their own top, the streets' cover is in each block's own kit, and the two faults round 22 named in batch 8 are fixed.** The ring road round the plaza is paved kerb to kerb in the plaza's stone with pale bands and cyan light strips set into it, and THE SPIRE's sign column is lit up its corners. Each High City block has its own crown: a stepped tank and spike on the north block, two billboards on the east, a crane on the west, planted balconies on the south; the lookout has a crate stack and a lattice mast on its rim, and the tower has balconies on three storeys. MARKET's and NOODLE ROW's concrete barriers are now food stalls, crate stacks and drinks machines, and two buildings in each corner block have open upper windows onto rooms you can reach from the street. The two long lanes underground are broken halfway by islands of vending machines and kiosks. THE STACKS has a deck in the middle of its court on pink pillars, bridged to the hotel galleries; THE WORKS' yellow containers have hazard stripes and warning labels. THE CANYON's walkways have a booth every 15 to 20 m and its roof is split into pads with gaps to jump; THE TERMINAL's vaults have ribbed glass and a catwalk along each row. Eight of LANTERN ALLEYS' houses have flat roof terraces joined by plank bridges hung with lanterns, with a fire escape up from the street. GLASS HEIGHTS' lift is a smoked glass shaft with a lit cab. The ceilings that round 22 read as olive mould are a clean even grey, and the rope frames are slim grey steel with a lit collar where the rope hangs. The street benches' lit strip is gone (it read as a panel lying on the ground), THE VAULT's doorway step is gold, and THE TERRACES' rooms and THE STRIP club's balconies have small flush ceiling lamps for the long strips that read as floating shelves.
