**Batch 8 (map version 141): THE VAULT's strongroom is a real room, the High City can be climbed, GLASS HEIGHTS has shops at street level, and the faults round 21 named are fixed.** The strongroom behind THE VAULT's round door now runs from floor to ceiling in dark steel trimmed gold, with five ways in instead of one: the door, a side door from each of the two halls beside it, and a drop into it from each hall on the floor above. Three of the four High City blocks have a stack of ledges up their outer face, 3 m apart from the street to the roof, so you can climb them. The base's roof has stair houses, chest-high walls and crates, and the Sky Ring's brick underside is lit. The streets round the centre have low barriers every 8 to 10 m, so the long lines down them are broken up. GLASS HEIGHTS' blank grey street storey is now seven shops with glass fronts, lit rooms and canopies, and its lift is one clear glass tube. THE WORKS' scattered crate piles are gone: its yard is a pipe rack and a crane over the containers, on concrete slabs with drains. LANTERN ALLEYS has a lantern pavilion round the mast in its court; THE TERRACES' rooms are plaster and timber; THE STACKS' planters are all pink. The city past the fence is darker by day and dimmer by night, so the play area is the brightest part of the view, and each district's own night colour is twice as strong. Every glass lift's top and zip line's end now hangs from a steel post, THE LOBBY's sign box in the atrium hangs from four rods, the NIGHT MARKET banner hangs above the walkway's fence instead of behind its posts, THE STRIP's crown is a dark cap with a magenta ring instead of a glowing disc, and the tiled ceilings that read as creased are plain plaster.
