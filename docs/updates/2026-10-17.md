# 2026-10-17

**THE STRIP lit up.** The club glows at night now: pink light strips run under its balcony, and lamps light the
dance floor that used to be pitch black. The street lamps by THE STRIP shine magenta.

**GLASS HEIGHTS lit up.** At night the gangways between the glass decks glow cyan underneath, and the long one over
the ring road from the North deck now reads as a line of light across the sky. The street lamps there are cyan too.

**THE CANYON, in the south-west.** The south-west corner has a district now: a canyon street between two cliffs of lit
windows 27 m high, running down both arms and round the corner, with passages in from the ring road. It is bare
inside for now. The walkways along its walls, bridges across the top and a market on its floor come next.

**Walkways up THE CANYON's walls.** Both walls of the canyon now carry a walkway 3.5 m up, with glass rails, all the
way round. Each wall has a stair set into it, and the stair runs straight up onto the walkway.

**THE CANYON's roofs.** Four bridges now cross the canyon at the top of its walls, joining the roofs on both sides. Jump
pads in the two passages throw you up onto the inner roofs. Every roof edge has a glass rail, and AC boxes and water
tanks stand on the roofs as cover.

**APUHTHEE's thumb stays out of its slide when you swap.** As a swap started, your right hand twisted on the pistol's
grip before letting go of it, and the thumb sank into the slide for a moment. It now turns as it lets go.

**THE CANYON's market and its light.** The canyon floor now has crates, tables and barrels down both walls under the
walkways, with food stands in the two passages. At night yellow-green strips glow under the walkways, lamps under them
light the floor, and street lamps line the roofs. The walkways are also now drawn at the height you walk on them:
since they went in they had been drawn half a metre low. THE CANYON is finished.

**A bad sign taken off the food stands.** One of the pack's food stands had a Korean neon sign on it that reads "free
sex", the sign already banned from the map. It stood in NOODLE ROW and LANTERN ALLEYS. Every food stand is now the
same stand without its signs, and the map's build now refuses any piece with a banned sign inside it.

**THE WORKS begins.** The empty south-east corner now has five big rusted-metal halls in an L: two down each side and a
tall works block in the corner. You cross the whole district indoors, through big doors from hall to hall, and the side
doors open onto the ring road and the edge. Inside, the halls are lit by ceiling lamps.

**Inside THE WORKS.** The four long halls now have steel columns, a gallery along the back wall with a stair up to it,
a big tank, engines, crates and a concrete barrier to fight round, and pipes and cables on the walls and ceiling.

**The works block's upper floor.** The tall corner block of THE WORKS now has an upper floor round an open atrium, with
jump pads in the middle that throw you up to it, rails round the drop, and cover along the walls.

**THE WORKS' roofs.** Jump pads in the yards between the halls now throw you onto the roofs, footbridges cross between
them, every roof edge has a rail, and each roof carries a big tank on legs you can duck under. The corner block is
crowned with the two biggest tanks, 31 m up, the district's landmark.

**THE WORKS is finished.** Teal lights now glow under the galleries and round the works block's atrium, teal lamps
stand on the roofs, and THE WORKS is written in teal facing the ring road. That completes all eight districts round
the centre.

**The centre's sixth review.** A fresh reviewer scored the centre again: 5.5 out of 10, the same as last time. It likes
the tower and the climbing round it, and asks for more on the ground, a richer VAULT and a quieter sky. First fixes: THE
VAULT's sign no longer loses letters, and the zip lines are now dark cables with small lights along them instead of
glowing tubes.

**The tower's corners.** Inside the tower, its rounded corners showed as shiny black panels that caught the lights. They
are now plain walls like the rest of each floor.

**ANAKIN's right hand holds its grip properly.** It used to hold the very bottom of the grip, with the trigger finger
stretched up to reach the trigger. Now the hand sits high on the grip, right behind the trigger, the way every other
gun is held. You'll see it in the inspect, the reload and the swap.

**THE VAULT glows gold.** Gold lights now run along the foot of THE VAULT's maze walls inside the tower.

**Tell B00G from the menu.** There is a Feedback tab now: say whether it is a bug, an idea or a change, write it and
send it, and it goes straight to B00G with the version you are on and your settings. And the game now notes how long
its loading took for you, so slow loads can be found and fixed.

**Taller cover in the streets.** The centre's streets now have more of the tall poster boards, about one in every two
pieces of road cover, and ticket machines on the pavements, so you can fight down a boulevard from cover to cover.

**The court under the tower.** The sunken court under THE VAULT has a polished black marble floor now (the grass is
gone), with stacks of crates and machinery to fight around.

**A Render scale setting for laptops.** Settings now has Render scale, next to Graphics: turn it down from 100% and
the world is drawn at fewer pixels and stretched to your screen, which can take a third off the graphics card's work.
The HUD and menus stay sharp. And on fast machines, bots far away no longer animate faster than anyone can see.

**A tip when the game is running slowly.** If your frame rate stays under 45 for a while, the game tells you once
what to try: Render scale or the Competitive preset in Settings.

**Cover on the gangway.** The long gangway from the tower out to the east deck has cover in a zigzag now: crate stacks
and machinery against one rail and then the other, each with a concrete shield beside it, so you can cross it under fire.

**The launcher is now called DEVINOS.** Same rocket launcher, new name, everywhere it shows up.

**The missing city, fixed and guarded.** For a few minutes this afternoon the battle royale's city did not load on
High: a release went out while the new map was still being copied in. It was put right minutes later, and now a
release cannot go out unless the city's files are all there and whole, and the check after every release looks at
them on the live site.

**Wall runs last 3 seconds.** A wall run now ends after 3 seconds, dropping away from the wall in its last moment,
instead of going on for as long as you hold it. Kick to another wall to keep going.
