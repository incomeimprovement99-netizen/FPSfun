# 2026-10-09

**Every gun checked on other players' soldiers.** All ten guns were looked at again on the soldier you see on other
players, in a dozen poses each. A BOOG player looking up no longer has their left arm through the rifle, and PANDA no
longer passes through the arm while swapping.

**The tower's doors look like doors.** Inside the big tower, the walls dividing each floor had plain gaps for doorways
that ran right up to the ceiling, so the walls looked like stage scenery. Each doorway now has a proper steel door frame
with a top over it, the wide gateways too, and the floor numbers by the stairs are bright on black so you can read them.
The lights on the highest floor of halls, which hung half a metre below its ceiling, are up where they belong.

**The bots' movement test looks past their looting.** A test that checks the bots move after they land was failing on
the Neon City. Watching each bot showed none was stuck: about half were picking up the first gun they found, just as the
test looked. The test now watches each bot for 12 seconds of game time instead of 6, and in every run since nearly all of
them have been on the move.

**HAEFY's rockets, fixed after a review.** A bot with HAEFY now fires real rockets: they hit you when they land,
not the moment they are fired, and a near miss still hurts. Other players' rockets burst on you instead of passing
through you, a rocket at someone's feet counts as a hit on them, the training range's targets take rocket damage, and a
rocket hit from very far away is no longer thrown out by the host. A burst no longer adds a light to the scene, which
made the game rebuild its shaders on every shot.
