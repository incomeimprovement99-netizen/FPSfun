# 3 October 2026

**The beams read.** MOTEL HILL's beam is really pink now (it drew nearly black), and the jump pads' white beams are a third
as tall, so only the four block beams reach the sky and you can tell at a glance which block you are looking at.

**The insides are lit.** The tower's floors, the big building round its foot, the court and the station are now lit from
their ceiling lamps, so you can see who is in there with you. Outside is as it was.

**Your player card stays up at least 5 seconds** when a battle royale starts, so you can read it.

**The game opens faster again.** The page no longer waits for your soldier to download before you can play: the range
opens first and the soldier arrives behind it. Old character files the game no longer uses are not downloaded at all.

**No more falling back to the old model.** If you open the game while an update is being put on the server, the
soldier's files can fail to come for a moment. The game used to give up straight away and show the old model for
the whole session. Now it tries again a few times over about 13 seconds, so you get the soldier.

**About two more seconds off the first load.** The game builds its graphics while it downloads instead of after, and
the code for playing with friends is only fetched when you start a match with friends.

**Cover on the decks and in the streets.** The four High City roofs now have air-conditioning units, chimneys and crates
to fight round, and the pavements of every street carry kiosks, planters, benches and billboards between the lamps:
something to put between you and a sniper on the way across.

**The loading screen is gone in about half the time.** The game now waits only for what you see first in the range:
the floor, the sky, the two guns in your hands and your arms. The other guns, the crates and barrels, and the
textures of the other maps stream in over the next second or two while you are already playing.

**The tower's floors have numbers, looks and a vault.** Each of the tower's eight new floors shows its number in lights at
the stair, wears its own walls and floor, and the maze floor at 32 m is THE VAULT: named on the map, the richest loot in
the centre, on that floor alone.

**Roof routes in Market and the Well.** Pads on Market's rooms roof and on the street by the Well throw you onto the
higher roofs beside them, and pads up there throw you on onto the bridge ring at 27 m: every corner block now has a way
from the street to the High City's rooftop circuit.

**SpeedKills by name everywhere.** The screen at the end of the range is now a proper SpeedKills manual: the
controls, every move, the ten guns, the ten hacks and how the battle royale works. The graffiti on the course and
arena walls says SPEEDKILLS, the menus describe the Neon City instead of the old map, and the win screen says LAST
SQUAD STANDING.

**Leftovers from the old game switched off.** Ring Consoles and the Storm Surge no longer turn up in a SpeedKills
battle royale (there is no ring, the city decays), Resurgence is gone from the rules box, the old Gun Run, Crown and
Search modes are gone from the Friends list, and the "Guns" rule now hands out SpeedKills guns.

**Other players now see PANDA held properly.** The rifle is the second of the eight new guns fitted on the soldier
other players see, after ANAKIN. Its hands are on the gun with the trigger finger on the trigger, and it is carried
properly standing, aiming, sprinting, jumping and swapping. The punch's hand now goes round the magazine instead of
through it. Like ANAKIN, its stock still runs along the right forearm when crouched.

**A faster frame in the Neon City.** The guns lying on the floor are drawn a kind at a time instead of one by one (130
draw calls a frame down to about a dozen), the city's decay no longer re-checks every wall each frame, and a lot of
work the frame repeated for nothing is gone. On a busy machine the street of a full match went from 66 to 80 fps.

**The old game's switch is gone.** Settings no longer offers the old B00G's Range game, a browser that had picked it
opens in SpeedKills again, and the Friends tab explains SpeedKills' own modes instead of the old game's.

**The keys panel costs less.** The list of keys on the right of the screen is drawn once and reused, not redrawn every
frame.

**Signs, landmarks, and the tower's floors no longer glaring.** Each corner block now has a big solid neon sign on a roof
facing the middle (MOTEL, a food sign over Noodle Row, COOLS over Market, holocall over the Well), and each High City roof
its own landmark: a billboard frame, a flat billboard, a parked flying car, a cypress. The tower's floors were lit so
brightly they washed out to white; they now sit at a fifth of the strength, and each floor's walls are their own colour,
yellow kept for THE VAULT. And a box of cover that had walled off the south deck's bridge is gone, with a rule that no
cover may cut a roof in two.

**The first screen comes up sooner.** Two pieces of start-up work are gone: the armory no longer builds stand-in guns
that the real ones replace a moment later, and the Neon City's interior lights are placed using its collision grid
instead of checking every wall in the city one by one.

**Other players now see BIGANTLER held properly.** The pump shotgun is the third new gun fitted on the soldier other
players see: the left hand round the pump, the trigger finger on the trigger, and carried cleanly when sprinting and
jumping. Its own reload, shells into the gate and a pump, comes once its first-person reload is made.
