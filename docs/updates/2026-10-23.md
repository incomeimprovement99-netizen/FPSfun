# 2026-10-23

**Roofs worth fighting on.** THE STACKS' capsule hotel roof and THE WORKS' hall roofs have tanks, cabinets and cooling
units across them instead of bare plates, and THE TERRACES' tiers have cypress trees.

**No more beams over loot.** Guns and hack cores on the floor no longer stand in tall beams of light; each keeps
its coloured circle on the ground. An echo, where a squad mate can be brought back, still has its beam.

**Your left arm out of the way.** On ANAKIN, PANDA, BIGANTLER, CHOOCH and DEVINOS your left forearm now comes
straight up from the bottom of the screen to the gun, the way Apex, EMPULSE and Hyper Scape hold theirs, so you see
your hand and a little forearm instead of a sleeve across the corner. The first time you draw PANDA, CHOOCH or DEVINOS,
your sleeve no longer fills the bottom of the screen.

**Pipes and lanterns.** Pipe runs cross THE WORKS' yards between its halls and its teal lights are brighter, and red
lantern lines hang across THE CANYON from wall to wall over its market floor.

**One finger in the trigger guard.** The soldier others see holds every gun with its index finger alone in the trigger
guard, the pad of its last joint on the trigger and the tip just past the trigger's left side, as the owner described
("some of your fingernail would be past the trigger, all the way on the left side of the trigger, but only a little
bit"). Before, PANDA's and ANAKIN's middle fingers rode up into the guard, the long guns' fingertips went 8 to 26 mm
through the trigger, and the pistols' curled back on its right.

- A new measure, `__triggerFit` in tools/figure-audit.js: the pad to the trigger's front face, how far the tip is past
  the trigger's left side, and how far each other finger is up inside the guard.
- tools/figure-grip.ts fits the trigger finger to it (pad on, tip 3 mm past, `grip.triggerPast`) and charges any other
  finger in the guard. Three right hands moved a few millimetres where the finger alone could not reach (PANDA, ANAKIN,
  STRYDER).
- The e2e's soldier hold test holds every gun to it, and caught what the first moves broke: DEVINOS's wrist past its
  limit looking down (its hand went back; the finger reaches from there) and STRYDER's left thumb in the right hand
  (the thumb bent clear).
- Every gun now: pad 0.1 to 1.4 mm off the trigger, tip 2.3 to 3.8 mm past, no other finger in the guard.
