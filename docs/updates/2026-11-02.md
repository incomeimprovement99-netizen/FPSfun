# 2026-11-02

**And their shadows.** A soldier you are scoped in on was drawn with no shadow past 60 metres, though the sun's shadows
reach across the whole map and every wall beside them had one. They now cast one, the same as everything around them.
Yesterday's note on this said the shadows would not reach that far anyway; it was wrong, and it is corrected.

**Bodies at ledges.** A soldier killed standing right at the edge of a roof or walkway used to lie with their legs sticking out flat
over the drop. They now land wholly on the roof, sliding back onto it as they fall if they have to.

**Lift ropes no longer run through people.** Riding one of the city's straight-up lift ropes, a soldier was drawn with the glowing rope
going up through their body and out of the top of their head. The rope is now in front of them, held over their head.

**The menu names your keys.** The welcome, the Settings hints and the voice line now show the keys you've bound, not the
defaults. The range's moving soldiers no longer stand inside the moving boards, and walking through the tutorial's gate no
longer resets a stand's fused hacks. On a large HUD, the quick chat and the carry card no longer run into other text.

**Out of walls the short way.** Yesterday's fix for being thrown along walls could, in a cluster of thin wall pieces, throw
a body ten metres into a building and leave it stuck there. A body now comes out at the nearest free spot, never more than
2.5 metres away, and can walk out if there is none that close. A controller lying on the desk no longer gives a mouse player
aim assist, and touching it no longer wipes the spray wall.

**THE TERRACES rebuilt, THE VAULT a strongroom.** From the ring road you now see THE TERRACES' grand stair, 20 m wide and lit, with
an arched gate and its name at the top, and gardens and pergolas on its tiers. THE VAULT has a real strongroom behind an open gold
door, with gold bars and a two-storey chamber, and the base under the tower has an atrium with stairs up through it. THE CANYON's
street has kiosk islands to fight round and booths on its walkways, THE WORKS' hall has a boiler, turbines and a gantry you can climb,
and THE STRIP has big letter signs and screens. And MOTEL HILL's rope finally takes you all the way up onto its roof.

**Cover you can fight round.** The cover in the streets and districts now comes in sets of three to five pieces, little L- and
U-shaped courts, stall rows and crate piles, instead of single boxes sprinkled about, so mid-range fights have angles. Each
district has its own ground underfoot and its own cover pieces, and the black boxes that hid players now show their colour.

**The killcam shows what happened.** Rounds in the replay now come out of the guns, not the shooter's eyes where you never
saw them, and in the colours you saw them in, not all enemy red. They no longer leave a second bullet hole, and a rocket in
the replay bursts on the figures in the replay, not on players standing there now. The guns you dropped as you went down no
longer lie at your feet before you fell, and a squad mate's ghost no longer stands beside their own figure.

**Bots take a moment for each new target.** A bot that switched from one player to another fired at the second one instantly,
already dead on target. It now has to react and settle its aim again, as it does for anyone it first sees. And a bot no
longer fires the very first instant of a Gulag fight, having watched you through the countdown. In the range, the flick
drill's targets no longer pop up behind the flippers and boards where your shots could not reach them.

**Smaller settings fixes.** On a French or other non-QWERTY keyboard, the game now names keys by what is printed on them.
A controller can skip the title card, and pressing A on a key in the Controls tab no longer gets stuck waiting for a key.
Clearing a crosshair box no longer makes the crosshair nearly invisible, and you can right-click to paste a friend's code.

**Stairs on a slow machine.** Running up a flight of stairs could stop you dead if the game hitched for a moment, and at
around 15 frames a second it stopped you on every stair. You now run up stairs the same at any frame rate.

**Care packages when the host leaves.** If the host left just as a care package landed, the package now still holds its
three items; it used to come down empty. Nor does a second package fall for the same ring.

**Start on a controller works again on the mode card.** This afternoon's update made a controller's Start, pressed while a
mode's title card was showing, only skip the card; you had to press it again to play. One press does both again.
