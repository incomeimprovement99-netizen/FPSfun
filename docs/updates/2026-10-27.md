# 2026-10-27

**Third person with a wall behind you.** In third person, a wall at your back pulls the camera in close, and your
soldier's head used to fill a third of the screen. Now your soldier thins out as the camera comes in and is gone once
it is 0.6 m from your eye. And once you are out of a match, the camera watching someone else stops short of the wall
behind them instead of sitting inside the building.

- Found by a new survey of everything a figure does (climbs, zip lines, wall runs, the skydive, interacts, running
  backwards, every emote) and two probes of the Neon City in third person.
- The survey's other finds are next: a zip line rider with no hand on the rope, the long gun vanishing for emotes and
  interacts, a long gun planted muzzle-down in a climb, and enemies who barely stand out from the street at some spots.

**Scoreboards out of the way.** In team deathmatch, free-for-all and Control the kills and deaths board no longer
sits on top of the keys list down the right: it goes beside it, or under it on a narrow screen, on its own panel. The
three-player duel's board no longer covers the kill feed either.

**Invisible squad mates.** A team mate who turns invisible now stays visible to their own squad, as they should; only
the other squads lose them. The faint shimmer an invisible player leaves also follows them properly when they switch
guns, and no longer shows during your killcam.

**Zip lines ridden by one hand.** An enemy coming down a zip line hangs from the rope by the left hand now, the gun in
the right, the legs hanging under them. Before, they stood in the air under the rope with the gun in both hands and no
hand on it. Every gun is checked: the knuckles within 2 cm of their hold on the rope.

**Smoother long sessions.** Each Heal area left a little graphics memory behind when it faded, about a hundred pieces
a battle royale from the bots alone; now it is freed. And the trails behind other players no longer cause a tiny
hitch the first time one appears while you ride the dropship.

**Guns on the back.** An enemy who emotes or loots now carries their long gun on their back, upright, as in the
skydive; before, it vanished and popped back into their hands after. A pistol is put away. Every long gun was put
through every emote: none of the soldier ends up inside the slung gun.

**Notices stay readable while you loot.** With two or more items in reach, the list of them no longer covers a notice
like "SQUAD WIPED"; the notice moves below it.

**Gulag fixes for friends.** A duo where one friend lost the Gulag while the other was already out could keep running
with nobody alive; it now ends. Someone leaving the match while in the Gulag no longer stops it from ending either.
Winning the Gulag clears the echo your first death left, so a later restore can't put you back at that old spot with
duplicate guns. The Gulag no longer pulls you in after the match has ended, the bots and care packages go to the
right place after a host hands over, and a player on another side can be pinged as an enemy.

**Fair placements when a squad holds the zone.** In a match with friends on separate sides, everyone used to get the
same placement when a squad captured the zone; now a side that was already out keeps the place it went out in.
