# 2026-10-04

**A battle royale on the city loads again.** Yesterday's loading change used a helper that broke in the live build, so
matches got stuck at the end of UNPACKING THE CITY. It is fixed, and loading changes are now tested on the live build's
kind before they go out.

**More tips while you load in.** The tips beside your player card are no longer cut off at four: the column fills to
the bottom (nine on a 1080p screen), and long names on the card stay on one line.

**The city downloads once, not every visit.** Your browser was throwing away the city's big file (79 to 137 MB) between
visits, so the first match of every visit downloaded it again. It is now kept, and a return visit goes straight to
unpacking it. The page's first download is also a quarter smaller: the city's collision comes in after the range is up.

**The USSO and BOOG held like Hyper Scape's.** Both guns sit where you picked them: a little nearer your eye, the front turned right, still pointing at the crosshair. The USSO's left hand now holds it low, just ahead of the trigger, as the Harpy is held, coming up from the bottom of the screen with only the hand and a little wrist showing. BOOG's left arm goes down out of the picture and its hand holds the fore-end ahead of the magazine. Reloads, the melee, the inspect, swaps and pickups all still keep the hands out of the guns.

**Golden hour is the default sky now**, and the sky files are a fifth of the size (the first screen waited for one).
The game's code also downloads about a quarter smaller: the server sends it compressed with brotli.

**Other players now see REZ held properly.** The auto shotgun is the fourth new gun fitted on the soldier other players
see: the left hand on the front of the gun ahead of its magazine, the trigger finger on the trigger, and carried
properly when standing, aiming, sprinting and jumping.

**A little less work for each far-away player.** Players more than 60 metres away no longer draw their eyes, which are
far smaller than a pixel at that distance. In a busy city street that saves about 18 drawing steps a frame.

**The big building, checked floor by floor.** Walked from the bottom up after the owner's play test. The tower's floors
had a ragged crack all round where the floor and ceiling met the outer wall, with the sky showing through; the floors now
reach the wall and the crack is gone. Every stair in the building was measured tread by tread and none runs into a
ceiling, and every open window can be shot and jumped through. At street level Noodle Row has a canopy of red lanterns
strung across its streets, Market has stacks of crates, and lamps ring the Well's shaft.

**The range builds faster.** Its walls, rails and course pieces are rounded boxes, and making each new size used to
take a slow general-purpose step; it is now worked out once and reused, a third of the time.

**A friends' match on the city no longer drops a slow side.** If one of you was still loading the city's walls when
the other joined, the match could end at once with "The host left the match." The waiting side now keeps the
connection alive and catches up when it is ready.

**The vault glows gold.** A third fresh review scored the centre 5 of 10. THE VAULT's storey now has a band of gold light
round the tower at its floor and its ceiling, so you can see from anywhere where the best loot is; the zip lines are
twice as thick; and the strip along the foot of the tower's walls is straight instead of jagged.

**Only SpeedKills.** Joining a friend whose page was still on the old game used to switch you into the old game, old
name and all, and the switch never got you into their match anyway. Now you are told their page is behind and asked to
have them reload and send a new code.

**The page opens a little faster again.** The robot figures in the range and on the courses were each built piece by
piece; now each kind is built once and copied, and the first screen goes about an eighth of a second sooner. Bots in a
match are made the same way, three times faster each.

**"No match with that code" fixed.** For a few minutes after an update, a friend joining your code could be told there
was no such match: if your page was slow to ask the server where to register the code, it quietly used a public
matchmaking server instead of ours, and your friend looked on ours. Your page now waits for our server's answer.

**The streets are harder to snipe down.** A new tool stands an eye at head height on every bit of street and looks 48
ways: on the old map, from nine street points in ten someone could see you from past 60 m. It was not empty blocks but
the roads themselves lining up into 200 m straights. Now low buildings stand where the ground was free, tall advertising
boards stand in the median, every second parked car is a hover van, and the edge road has parked cars: the share of
directions you can be seen from far off fell from 8.1% to 4.5% on the curves and from 13% to 7.3% on the Loop.

**CHOOCH held properly, and other players see it overheat.** The energy LMG is the fifth new gun fitted on the soldier
other players see. When it overheats, other players now see the soldier tip the gun up and hold it while it cools,
instead of pretending to change a magazine it does not have. Bots carrying CHOOCH do the same.

**Swapping no longer pushes the gun into your hands.** While the gun phased out and the next one in, your cupped hands stayed still as the gun kept gently breathing, and BOOG could sway a few millimetres into your fingers. The hands now move with the gun.

**APUHTHEE turned the right way round.** The heavy pistol was being held backwards, by you and by everyone else: aimed,
you were looking down its barrel. The tool that measures the bought guns decides which end is the front from where the
model's centre point sits, and on this pistol that point is almost exactly in the middle, so it guessed wrong by 2 mm.
It now looks at where the grip hangs when the centre point cannot tell, and only this pistol changed.
