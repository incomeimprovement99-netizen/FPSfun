# 2026-10-12

**Up onto LANTERN ALLEYS' roofs.** Jump pads in the alleys and the back lanes now throw you up onto the district's
taller houses, and from there you can run and jump along the rooftops, with boxes and crates up there to take cover
behind.

**The old heal items are gone.** SpeedKills heals with its HEAL hack, and your health and shield come back on their
own. Underneath, the code still carried the old game's shield cells, syringes, batteries and med kits, the heal wheel,
and the backpacks that let you carry more of them, none of which you could ever pick up. They are out. Two things you
might notice: the 4 key and a controller's D-pad up do nothing now, and the death recap no longer has a line for
whether your killer healed just before.

**And the soldier's healing pose with them.** With no heal items left, nobody is ever seen healing, so the animation of
a soldier lowering its gun to use one, and the item it held, are gone too. Nothing you see changes.

**More of the old battle royale's rules are gone.** The old game's battle royale had four extras SpeedKills never
used: a locked vault with a guard and a keycard, Ring Consoles that showed where the ring went next, Storm Surge,
which hurt whoever had done the least damage late in a match, and a loadout crate that dropped your saved guns. All
four were switched off in SpeedKills and are now out of the code. Nothing changes in play. One loading screen tip about
the consoles is gone too.

**A fix for squads when the host leaves.** If whoever started a battle royale with friends drops out, the match carries
on with one of the others as the new host. A bot that had picked something up in the last second before that could
come out the other side without it, and the item was gone from the map. It now keeps it.

**The old game's floor loot is gone.** In SpeedKills the floor only ever has guns and hack cores, but the code still
carried the old game's ammo boxes, attachments, magazines, helmets and knockdown shields, and the rules for laying
them out. They are out. Nothing changes in play: the bots still loot guns, and you still hold the interact key to take
everything in a spot.

**A new district: THE TERRACES.** West of the city, past the ring road, the empty ground between the two roads out to
the edge now steps up in three garden terraces to the city's edge. A wide grand stair climbs the middle through a gate
with the district's name in green, and ramps zig-zag up each side. Shops are set into the terrace walls, glass rails run
along every edge, and there are cypresses, planters, benches and lamps. The top terrace looks out over the whole city.

**The old armour is gone too.** The old game's shield that grew as you did damage, and its helmets, were still in the code
under SpeedKills' one shield for everyone. They are out; nothing changes in play.

**The old map is gone.** The old game's battle royale map, Outskirts (a desert with a hub, a ridge and a town), was
still in the code, along with its rocks, dead trees and building trim, though SpeedKills only ever plays the Neon City.
It is out, which also takes about 13 MB of its models off the server. Nothing changes in play.

**The old knockdowns are gone.** In SpeedKills nobody is ever knocked down: at zero health you go to the Gulag, then
become a ghost. The old game's downs, bleed-out, revives, knockdown shield and finishers were still in the code, and
they are out. One thing this fixes: shooting a friend's squad member to zero in a squads match used to say KNOCKED DOWN
for a moment though they were already out. It says ELIMINATED now.

**And the soldier's knocked-down poses with them.** With no knockdowns left, nobody is ever seen crawling, reviving a
friend or throwing a finisher, so those animations are out of the soldier too, along with the glowing knockdown shield
and the DOWN state on the squad panel. Nothing changes in play. A friend on an older page that still sends one of those
moves is shown standing as usual.

**Wall running, rebuilt after EMPULSE.** A wall run used to last a second and a half while you slid down the wall,
and the next wall took most of your speed away. Now you run a wall as long as you hold forward along it, never sinking,
and you carry your speed onto the next wall, round an inside corner, and through each kick, which goes where you look
and adds a little burst like EMPULSE's. The view leans further into the wall (15 degrees, was 10) and eases from one
wall to the next instead of snapping straight. On THE CHAIN in the range, the wall gap can now be crossed just by running
the wall, and in the chimney, look across at the other wall as you kick.

**The game loads faster: the animations are a third of the size.** Every player character moves with a set of
motion-captured animations that every player downloads on the way in: 6.5 MB of them. Nine were never played (most
belonged to the old heals and knockdowns), and three quarters of the rest was the same number written down again for
every frame of a bone that never moves. Both are out, and the files are 2.2 MB now. The animations look exactly the
same: compared frame by frame, nothing moved by more than a few millionths.

**And the server now compresses them.** The game's server was sending every 3D model uncompressed. It now squeezes the
character animations, the soldier and the guns on the way out, so the animations that were 6.5 MB this morning now
take 0.8 MB to download, and the soldier half of what it did. The city map's files are left alone for now: they are
far bigger, and squeezing them on every visit would load the server.

**Old settings tidied away.** The old game's switches are gone from the code: the sprint setting (SpeedKills always
sprints), the movement box (the double jump and wall run are always on) and the "Battle royale sky" choice (a match
plays under the time of day you picked). Nothing changes in play; the settings you have kept are kept.

**THE TERRACES has blocks on top.** Along the top terrace by the city's edge there are now five blocks of flats with
balconies, stepping up to an 18 m roof behind the gate. You can climb onto the first one, jump the narrow gaps and
climb your way up to the top roof, then drop from roof to roof down the other side. There are crates and AC boxes up
there for cover.

**And the last of the old game's words.** The old name and its rules list were still hidden in the page, and a
hidden aim bot box in the match setup copied the one on the Settings tab. They are gone; the aim bot stays on the
Settings tab.
