# 30 September 2026

**Enemies reloading and swapping.** When an enemy reloads, the fresh magazine they pull from the pouch at their hip no
longer passes through their forearm: it sits in the hand the way you would hold it, then turns upright as it goes into
the gun. Switching weapons, the gun they lower now clears their body standing still as well as when they sprint.

**The Loadouts soldier stands normally.** On the Loadouts tab your soldier no longer stands squinting down the sights
with BOOG's scope flaring across the picture: they hold the gun ready at the shoulder, head up. The whole gun stays in
the picture as it turns; with BOOG the view steps back a little when the rifle swings round side-on.

**Enemies jumping out of a sprint.** A sprinting enemy who jumps now keeps their gun carried low through the jump and
the landing, the way a runner does, instead of snapping it up to the shoulder in the air and back down as they land.
When they stop running, the gun swings up to the shoulder clear of their arm.

**Enemies hit you with the gun, and five things you would have seen go wrong are fixed.** A bug hunt through real
matches, with the camera on bots, on another player over the network and on your own soldier in third person:

- A melee used to be a boxer's punch: the enemy's gun vanished, they lunged half a metre sideways, and the gun popped
  back through their hands. Now they strike with the gun itself, both hands on it, in the same time your own melee takes.
- After a grenade throw the gun no longer reappears through the hands: the hands come back first, then the gun is in them.
- Bots aim while they run, and their left hand was sinking into the gun the whole time. It stays on the grip now.
- Bots in SpeedKills were stopping to use healing items you do not have there, standing still with nothing in their
  hands. They no longer do; a hurt bot still ducks into cover while its shield comes back.
- When anyone swapped weapons, you or another player, the whole figure was being replaced, which showed as a one-frame
  pop. It is the same figure taking the new gun now.

**Bridges between the rooftops.** On the new map the four big rooftops round the tower are now joined by bridges that
cross high over the street corners, so you can run the whole way round the tower at roof height without coming down.
Each bridge has a short ramp onto it from the roof at either end. Two of the jump pads up to the north and west roofs
have moved a little along their buildings to make room for where the bridges land.

**The melee, tidied.** Two of the three strikes had the enemy's left hand slipping off the front of the gun and into it
at full stretch. They now lunge with the left shoulder forward, the way you would thrust with a rifle, and both hands
stay on the gun through every strike.

**The USSO held properly.** Your left hand now lies against the side of the USSO, palm and thumb on the gun, where there
was a gap you could see between them. Your right hand's middle finger now sits with the other two on the grip instead
of apart from them, with the index finger on the trigger.

**From the tower's courtyard down to the metro.** The sunken courtyard round the tower now has a way straight down to
the metro station: a door in its south wall opens onto a short corridor, then a ramp down to a gate into the station's
marble hall, and on to the platform. No more going up to the street and round to get from one to the other. The
courtyard's four corners had narrow gaps you could see the sky through; those are closed.

**A noodle bar and a gadget shop under the tower.** The two short corridors off the tower's sunken courtyard, east
and west, used to stop at a blank wall after a few steps. They now run on as proper underground shopping corridors:
a noodle bar with red neon, lanterns and a counter to the east, and an electronics shop full of screens to the west,
both open to walk into.

**Into a match, in the right order.** Starting a match now goes: the loading screen and its bar the moment you press
Start, the mode's card over it once everything is ready, and then the match. In a battle royale you are already on the
ship when the card breaks, and the ship waits for you: it only sets off once the card has gone. No more card over the
firing range, no flash of your gun on a black screen, and the few-second freeze the first time the city came into view
from the ship now happens while the game's first loading screen is up instead.

**The new map's middle has curved streets.** The streets around the tower no longer run in a straight grid. A round
road circles the tower, and eight streets wind out from it in S-bends to the straight roads beyond, with rounded
kerbs where they meet. The buildings along them are turned to follow the bends, each corner block has a wedge-shaped
building facing its junction, and the four tall rooftop blocks have moved out to face the round road. The rest of the
map keeps its straight roads, so the middle looks and plays differently from the outskirts.

**The soldier reloads the way you do, holds the trigger, and flips on a double jump; and a pistol in the ten.** From
your look at the USSO and BOOG:

- An enemy's reload is now the same as the one in your own hands, on the same clock: the left hand points at the
  magazine as it slides out and phases away in orange cubes, a new one phases in and snaps home, then the USSO's
  charging handle is racked or BOOG's bolt worked. No more dropped magazine and no trip to a hip pouch, which your own
  view never had. The two views now read their timing from one place, so they cannot drift apart.
- The trigger finger is on the trigger, not laid along the side of the gun.
- The fingers are a tenth smaller than the model's. Half size could not hold the guns (the trigger finger could not
  reach BOOG's trigger and the left hands closed into fists beside the guns), and thinner fingers looked wrong.
- BOOG is held by the rail under its barrel, not by its magazine, and the hand stays there aimed on the run.
- A double jump is a front flip, gun and all, for you in third person and for everyone who sees you do it.
- PULSAR is gone. HAEFY is the one Marksman, and in PULSAR's place is HAMMER (a working name), a heavy pistol like
  Hyper Scape's Riot One: six rounds, no drop-off, almost no recoil, five body hits a kill. It wears the pack's boxy
  second pistol with its own red dot.

**A raised walkway round the tower.** On the new map a walkway one floor up now rings the plaza round the central
tower, with glass railings that glow green at night and four staircases up from the plaza. Walk the whole way round
above the road, or use it to look down on the ring road and the streets curving away from it.

**No more flip; a knee lift, a streak of light, and a glint only when scoped.** The double jump's front flip made anyone
double jumping (which is everyone) hard to read and hit, so now their knees just come up for a moment as they jump
again. If the double jump turns them, a streak of their trail's colour hangs in the air along the turn for most of a
second, so you can see where they went. And a sniper's scope only glints while they are actually looking down it, not
while they reload or swap.

**The tower now stands on a wide base.** On the new map the central tower rises out of a big three-storey block that
fills most of the plaza inside the round road. Walk in through any of fourteen doors, or cross from the raised walkway
by one of four bridges onto its first floor. The ground floor is a concourse with shops along its sides and the metro
entrances in the middle, the first floor is a maze of offices, the second a warehouse of racks and pillars, and the
roof is open with cooling units to hide behind. Each floor has four staircases, and the ones up are never next to the
ones you came up, so every floor is crossed on the way to the top.

**A bug hunt through every mode.** We played every mode automatically, start to finish, with a watchdog looking for
anything wrong, and fixed what it found:

- Battle royale bots no longer freeze in place for the rest of a match. Some stood under a walkway they could not
  reach, and others stood inside a care package. Now they carry on, and they fight around a care package instead of
  standing in it.
- Going into the Gulag no longer freezes the game for most of a second.
- With the music turned down to nothing (the default), the game no longer streams the music in the background.
- The soldier model is downloaded once instead of twice.

**Empty and tactical reloads told apart, the trigger finger round the trigger, and swaps that phase.** From your
look at the USSO and BOOG:

- A reload from an empty magazine and one with a round still in the chamber are now told apart, for you and for
  everyone watching you. From empty, the soldier does the whole reload: the magazine out and in, then the USSO's
  handle racked or BOOG's bolt worked, over the longer empty time. With a round still chambered it is the quicker
  tactical reload. The guns side is finishing your own view's tactical reload (the magazine only, no rack). When it
  lands, the soldier others see plays it too, with nothing more to change on this side.
- Bots always reload from empty, and now take the empty reload's time, as you would.
- In squads, your friends used to see the host's bots stand idle while they reloaded, healed or aimed. Now everyone
  sees the same bot doing the same thing.
- The trigger finger now goes through the guard and wraps the trigger at the crease of its last joint. On BOOG only
  the very tip was on the trigger; that is fixed. The USSO's finger was nearly there and now curls further round.
- A gun swap on the soldier now looks like the one in your hands. The gun being put away dissolves into the orange
  cubes from the muzzle back, and the new one builds up from its stock to its muzzle. Before, the new gun just
  appeared in the hands halfway through.
- Frame by frame: the USSO's left wrist no longer bends sharply while it points at the magazine. BOOG's right hand
  sits better on its bolt. When a gun is lowered for a swap, the USSO's magazine no longer sinks into the soldier's
  belly, and BOOG's stock goes less far into the chest.
- Still to fix in the next pass: the USSO's left hand dips into the gun at the charging handle, BOOG's right hand dips
  into it at the bolt, and BOOG's long stock still cuts into the right arm in a sprint jump.

**The hand on the USSO's charging handle holds it.** When the soldier racks the USSO, the left hand's fingers now
hook over the handle instead of hanging in the shape they had along the front of the gun. BOOG's hand on the bolt
was reshaped the same way. The palm still sinks a little into the gun at the handle; that is next.

**The guns held like Apex, Hyper Scape and EMPULSE hold theirs.** The USSO and BOOG now come up out of the bottom right
corner, nearer and bigger in the picture, turned in toward your crosshair, and your support arm is only a forearm and
hand under the gun instead of an arm across the lower left of the screen. We measured where those games put their guns
on the screen and fitted ours to them. Your hands hold the guns just as before, and aiming down the sights is unchanged.

**Swapping guns, in place.** A swap no longer throws the gun up into the air. The gun stays in your hands and breaks up
from its edges inward while your hands ease off it and cup round it, fingers curled, like holding a Hadouken; then the
next gun builds out from the middle and your hands settle onto it. The swap takes a little longer so you can see it.

**The melee.** With the USSO or BOOG out, your right hand keeps hold of the gun and your left pulls back beside you and
punches. It hits for 30 as before, and the gun cannot fire during the punch.

**Hands and small things.** BOOG's right hand now wraps its grip with the three lower fingers together and the index
finger on the trigger, and the USSO's index finger rests on its trigger too. On a reload the new magazine now builds from
the top down in place instead of rising into the gun. Inspecting a gun makes it glow amber round its edges, like the hack
cards, in place of the old light band.

**Reloading with a round still in the gun.** Reloading the USSO or BOOG before the magazine is empty now skips the
charging handle and the bolt, since a round is already chambered: the magazine swaps and your hand goes back to the gun.
From empty you still rack it. The soldier other players see does the same.
