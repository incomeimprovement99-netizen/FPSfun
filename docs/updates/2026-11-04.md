# 2026-11-04

**Guns on the back.** Every soldier now carries the gun it isn't holding on its back, as Apex's legends do, so you can
read an enemy's whole loadout at a glance. Put your gun away and it goes onto your back too, with your hands empty. A swap
trades the two, each one fading out of one place and into the other.

**DEVINOS without the jumps.** Starting a slide, or finishing a reload, with DEVINOS made the launcher jump across the
soldier's body in a single frame. It now swings there smoothly, about half as far in any one frame.

**Hits push the right way.** A soldier you shoot in the back now jolts forward, and one shot from the side jolts
sideways. Before, every hit made them lean back, even toward the shots.

**Friends start running smoothly.** When a friend who was standing still started to move, their soldier on your screen
stayed put for a moment, then jumped forward and paused. It now sets off smoothly, the way they really did.

**Your recent matches.** The Stats tab now lists your last 20 matches. Each one shows when you played, the mode, how it went,
your kills and damage, and how long you lasted. It stays with your account if you sign in.

**Bots react to where hits came from.** A bot shot from the side or the back by a friend now flinches away from the shot, on
the host's screen as well.

**A kill leader in the battle royale.** Once someone has three kills and the most in the match, everyone is told who the
kill leader is, on screen and by the announcer. Take them out and the whole match hears about it. It can be you, a friend or
a bot.

**Your damage and your connection on screen.** In a battle royale, the damage you have dealt now shows under your kills.
In a match with friends, your ping and how many packets are being lost show under the FPS. They turn yellow, then red,
when the connection gets bad.

**Bodies fall the right way.** A soldier killed high up used to go flat while still in the air, then drop like a plank. Now
the body starts to fall the moment it's hit and lies flat just as it reaches the ground.

**Friends on ledges are drawn where they stand.** A friend who stepped up onto something and stood still could be drawn up to
9 m above where they really were, for up to two seconds.

**Bodies no longer hang in the air.** A soldier killed in mid-air stayed there, lying in the air, for the rest of the match.
That happened to anyone killed falling off a ledge, riding a rope or jumping, and every player saw it. Now the body falls to
the floor below. A bot killed on a rope also no longer comes back on the rope after it respawns.
