# 2026-11-04

**Guns on the back.** Every soldier now carries the gun it isn't holding on its back, as Apex's legends do, so you can
read an enemy's whole loadout at a glance. Put your gun away and it goes onto your back too, with your hands empty. A swap
trades the two, each one fading out of one place and into the other.

**DEVINOS without the jumps.** Starting a slide, or finishing a reload, with DEVINOS made the launcher jump across the
soldier's body in a single frame. It now swings there smoothly, about half as far in any one frame.

**Hits push the right way.** A soldier you shoot in the back now jolts forward, and one shot from the side jolts
sideways. Before, every hit made them lean back, even toward the shots.

**Friends start running smoothly.** When a friend who was standing still started to move, their soldier on your screen
stayed put for a moment, then jumped forward and paused. It now sets off smoothly, the way they really did.
