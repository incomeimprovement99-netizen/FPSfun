# 28 September 2026

**Reloads you can watch, and arms that get out of the way.** Reloading the USSO or BOOG, the gun now turns toward you
and your left hand points at the magazine while it burns out and a new one burns in, then the hands work the gun: the
USSO's charging handle gets smacked home, BOOG's bolt gets thrown. Switching weapons, the arms now drop out of the
picture as the old gun burns away and come back up with the new one, instead of hanging in the air.

**BOOG held with both hands again.** A few seconds after drawing BOOG its right arm had been drifting off out of sight,
leaving the rifle held by one hand. That was a bug on the live server, and it is fixed.

**The bought buildings' real faces on the centre's towers.** The city packs don't come with whole buildings: each
pack's buildings exist only in its demo street, assembled piece by piece by the artist. The game now cuts those demo
streets into their building faces, balconies, fire escapes, pipes, AC units and signs included, and stacks them up the
centre's towers, so neighbouring stretches end at different heights the way a real street's buildings do. From
Balanced up, 44% of the centre's street faces now wear them, costing about 2 ms a frame on the owner's machine. Their
densest cables and pipes were thinned where you would never see the difference, which cut Kyber's share by more than
a third.

**A haze with a colour.** From Balanced up, the air takes the colour of the part of the city you are in (green in
Cyber City, warm in Kyber, pale in High City, blue round the Spire), thins as you climb, and the streets are wet and
reflect the neon. Competitive keeps its clear air.

**Halls to fight in at street level.** The big blocks under the centre's towers are no longer solid: each of the
eight is a hall you can run into from the street through doors on every side, as the metro is below. Inside are
columns and waist-high counters to fight round, shop fronts along the walls (from Balanced up), and lights in the
ceiling. The roofs on top are just as they were. Bots walk in and out of them, and each hall has loot of its own; the
loot everywhere else is exactly where it was.

**More of the bought textures in place.** Parts of the bought models that were showing plain grey now wear their real
materials, the flying cars' engines among them. Competitive players also download half as much for the city.

**Jump pads that look like part of the city.** The big gold discs and fat beams are gone. A pad is now a vent in the
floor, a dark grate with a thin ring of its colour round it (and, in the centre from Balanced up, a real metal vent
from the bought kit), a slimmer beam, and one ring where it lands you. They still show from a street away.

**A gallery in every hall.** The halls now have a walkway round their walls a storey up, with two staircases up to
it, so there is high ground inside to fight from. Each hall also has three kiosks in the middle of the floor, dressed
as street food stands from the bought kits, to use as cover.

**The USSO and BOOG checked frame by frame.** Every stage of the reload, the weapon swap, aiming and picking things up
is now photographed and measured every few percent, looking for anything wrong: hands through the gun, bent wrists, a
finger pointing off target. What that found and fixed:

- The pointing finger now lands exactly on the magazine, a little down and to the left of it, instead of 4 cm off.
- The old magazine drops out of the gun as it burns away, and the new one burns in below and slides up into place.
- The USSO's charging handle: the pack's animation is made for a gun with the handle at the front, and on ours it threw
  the left hand up in front of your face for a fifth of the reload. The left hand now grabs the handle at the back,
  pulls it and lets it slam home.
- A live bug: the second time you drew either gun, it sat wrong in the hands, the left palm off the gun. Fixed.
- The hands fitted to our guns' grips, which are thicker than the pack's, so fingers do not sink into them.

**The pack's own moves.** Switching guns now plays the arms pack's animation: the gun is swung down to your chest in
both hands as it burns away, and the next one brought up into the hold as it burns in. Jumping plays its take-off and
landing, and picking up loot has the left hand reach down for it.

**A corner of the city that is High City's own street.** The map's south-east corner is now High City's demo street
exactly as its artist built it: every building, walkway, sign and parked flying car where the pack puts them, in its
own misty grey-green air, with its windows dim by day and its canyons in its buildings' shadow. It answers the
question of whether the bought assets come out whole: they do. From the same camera, the game draws what the pack's
own scene draws. The city looked unlike the store because we were laying the pieces over a map drawn before them.

**And you can play it.** Walk into its canyons from the streets round it. Five jump pads throw you up to the walkways
either side, and the buildings are solid behind their faces, so there is no getting behind the scenery to see through
it. The stray orange line that ran across the canyon floor is gone. To try it at once, open the game with
`?dropat=high-corner` on the address and start a solo battle royale: you drop straight onto its crossroads. It has loot
of its own, but the bots don't go in yet.

**Bots in High City's corner, and solid lamp posts.** Bots now walk into the new district's canyons, and its pads
take them up to the walkways too. Lamp posts, bins and crates on the walkways now stop you where you see them. They
used to be invisible walls 40 m high, or ledges floating just off the ground that caught your feet.

**No more white slabs in High City's corner.** The plain grey blocks showing between its buildings and over its roofs
were one of the pack's background buildings that came out without its texture. It now wears its brick and lit windows.

**The Gulag's bot comes for you.** It used to stand on its spawn for the whole fight. Now, when the 3 second countdown
ends, it heads for you.

**The USSO and BOOG, closer to perfect.** Standing still, both guns now point straight ahead instead of up and to the
left. The USSO's left hand now rests on the gun instead of just beside it, and when you rack it the thumb and forefinger
actually pinch the charging handle and pull it. Holstering and meleeing now use the new bought arms and gloves, not the
old fists. And inspecting a gun, your other hand opens palm up with the hack you carry floating over it.

**The out-of-bounds laser is gone.** Stay past the city's edge and the countdown still runs, and at zero it is out of
bounds, but there is no red laser and no red ring on the ground any more.

**Your loadout on the Battle Royale screen.** Under the Start and With friends buttons there is now a card showing the
loadout you will play with: its two guns, your operator and your heirloom, with a Change button that takes you to the
Loadouts tab.
