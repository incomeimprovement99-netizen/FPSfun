# The centre, reviewed: scores, the gap to Hyper Scape, and what raises them

The owner, 2026-10-02: "give this middle area a rating ... do the gap analysis between Hyperscape ... and then think of
ways to improve that area ... so that, don't be biased, ... if I gave a fresh agent this and it didn't look at your code
... it just looked at images and layouts ... that it would think it's pretty good too."

**How it is scored.** The centre is photographed from the same 24 spots (from above, two aerials, street level, the
plaza, the base's hall and roof, the Sky Ring, the lookout, three tower floors, a High City roof, the bridge ring, three
corner blocks, the court, the station, a walk-in building, and two at night). A fresh agent that has never seen the code
or the docs gets only those pictures and a short brief on the game and on Hyper Scape. It scores what it sees, out of
10, where 10 is a shipped AAA map and 5 a competent prototype. The same spots and brief are used each time, so the scores
can be compared from one round to the next.

Ours is higher on finish because 102 automated checks walk every stair, pad, lift, zip line, door and bridge. What a
player sees is weaker than what works.

**What drags it down:** the interiors are near-black at noon (the tower's floors, the base's hall, the court); the
tower's floors are sealed boxes with no windows; there is little mid-range cover on the roofs and streets; the four High
City blocks look identical; the corner blocks do not show their names from the street; a dozen white pad beams clutter
the sky and MOTEL HILL's pink beam draws nearly black.

**What already works:** the tower is a landmark you see from everywhere; the levels stack (station, court, street, Sky
Ring, base roof, tower floors, bridge ring, lookout); long range is well served (the decks, the lookout, the Sky Ring and
the streets) and close range too (eight tower floors, the base, the court, the metro, walk-in buildings).

### The gap to Hyper Scape (Neo Arcadia, and Red Tiger at its centre)

- **The landmark.** Red Tiger is a cluster of ornate, distinct buildings with the best loot on the map, and it respawns
  there. Our tower reads from anywhere, but nothing says the best loot is here: no vault, no gold chests.
- **The city.** Neo Arcadia is continuous: every block three storeys or more, its streets canyons. Ours is dense within
  about 100 m of the tower; past that the corner blocks are a few low buildings across wide roads.
- **Getting around.** Hyper Scape has a jump pad on every block, stairs to every roof, and roofs close enough to jump
  between. Ours has three roof tiers (10.5, 26.9 and 49 m) with gaps too wide to jump and nothing between them.
- **Fights.** Hyper Scape's interiors have windows onto the street, so inside fights outside. Ours had none.
- **Loot.** Theirs lies on floors and in breakable crates, concentrated in the landmarks. Ours is spread by tier, with
  nothing breakable.

### What raises it, in order

1. Open windows in the tower's floors and the base (the owner's ask). *Done, Milestone 417.*
2. Two pads to the tower's roof at 108 m from the base's roof, with cover up there and on the lookout: a superior spot
   anyone can contest. *Done, Milestone 420.*
3. Light the interiors. *Done, Milestone 425.*
4. Fix the beams: MOTEL HILL's black beam, and shorter pad beams so only the four block beams stand tall. *Done,
   Milestone 424.*
5. Cover on the roofs, and mid-range cover in the streets. *Done, Milestone 429: the decks and the pavements; the
   roofs of the corner blocks' buildings still to come.*
6. Each tower floor a theme and a big floor number; one a vault floor with the best loot: our Red Tiger. *Done,
   Milestone 431.*
7. The corner blocks' identities visible from the street. *Done, Milestone 437: a sign each.*
8. The four High City blocks each their own look. *Done, Milestone 437: a landmark each.*
9. A middle roof tier you can jump between, and more pads. *Done, Milestone 432: the tier was there; Market's and the
   Well's routes up to the bridge ring added, as Motel Hill and Noodle Row had.*
10. Buildings between the Loop and the corner blocks, so the streets are canyons.

After every few of these the centre is photographed and scored again by a fresh agent, and the round is added here.
