# SpeedKills

SpeedKills is a fast movement shooter that runs in your browser. Fight across the Neon City in a battle royale where
the city itself decays around you, or in quick 1v1s, team fights and Control. Everything here, the range included, is
free to play and needs nothing installed.

Shoot the arrows beside this screen to turn the page or change section, or punch a section's name in the list.

## Controls

| Key | Action | Key | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| W A S D | Move | Space | Jump (twice: double jump) |
| Shift | Sprint | Ctrl or C | Crouch, slide |
| Left click | Fire | Right click | Aim |
| R | Reload | I | Inspect your gun |
| Q or Mouse 5 | Swap guns | E | Pick up, use |
| F | Mobility hack | G | Utility hack |
| V | Melee | Middle click | Ping (hold: the wheel) |
| M | Map | 7 | Emotes (hold) |
| X | Third person | / | Show or hide the keys |

Every key can be changed in Settings, Controls. A controller works too.

## Movement

- **Double jump:** jump again in the air.
- **Slide:** crouch while you sprint, and jump out of it to keep its speed.
- **Wall run:** jump at a wall and hold forward along it.
- **Wall kick:** jump into a wall and jump again off it.
- **Mantle:** jump at a ledge and you climb it.
- **Superglide:** jump in the last moment of a mantle and crouch the frame after for a burst of speed. Every mantle in
  the range draws a timing bar under the crosshair.
- **Tap-strafe:** just after a jump, tap a direction key to swing your momentum that way.
- **Jump pads, lifts and zip lines** carry you onto the roofs and up the towers. Ride a zip line with E.

## Guns

Ten guns, two in your hands at a time. You never run out of ammo: reload and keep moving.

- **USSO**, fast SMG, and **ANAKIN**, steady SMG: close range, fast fire.
- **STRYDER**, fast rifle, and **PANDA**, heavy rifle: steady at any range.
- **BIGANTLER**, heavy shotgun, and **REZ**, auto shotgun: point blank, one or two shots.
- **HAEFY**, marksman: long range, hard single shots.
- **BOOG**, sniper: the longest range, and a headshot kills.
- **APUHTHEE**, heavy pistol: six hard single shots.
- **CHOOCH**, energy LMG: never reloads, it cools instead.

**Fusion:** take a copy of a gun you carry and it fuses a level up, to level 5: a bigger magazine, faster reloads,
more damage and less recoil. A copy at a higher level takes yours straight to it.

## Hacks

You carry one mobility hack (F) and one utility hack (G). Take the same hack again to fuse it a level up, to level 4:
a shorter cooldown each time.

- **DASH:** a fast blink the way you look.
- **SLAM:** up, then down onto a spot, hurting whoever is under it.
- **LEAP:** straight up about four storeys, then glide.
- **GRAPPLE:** a line that pulls you to a surface.
- **HEAL:** a healing area for you and your squad.
- **ARMOR:** take much less damage for a moment, moving slower.
- **WALL:** a barrier where you aim.
- **INVISIBILITY:** nearly unseen for a few seconds. Firing ends it.
- **REVEAL:** shows enemies in front of you through walls.
- **MINE:** a mine that homes in on an enemy close by.

## Battle royale

- **The drop:** the ship flies over the Neon City. Jump when the doors open, steer down, and land where you like. The
  middle is the hottest drop: the most loot, and the most fights.
- **The decay:** there is no ring. Sectors of the city decay in waves, each warned on your map first. Inside a
  decaying sector you take damage, and it eats the ground floors first: climb or get out.
- **The capture zone:** when only the last sector is left, its capture zone opens. Hold it to win, or be the last
  squad standing.
- **Second chance:** die once and you fight your way back in a 1v1. Die again and you are a ghost: stay near your
  squad and they can restore you at your echo, faster with you beside them.
- **Squads:** solo, duos or trios, against bots, with friends on your side.

## Other modes

On the Play tab, behind **Extra modes**.

- **1v1 and 1v1v1:** with a friend or against a bot. A 1v1 is fought on THE CENTRE, the Neon City's own middle, inside
  a circle you cannot leave.
- **Arena, bots:** rounds against bots in an arena.
- **Team Deathmatch, Control and Free-for-all:** with friends, bots or both.
- **Friends:** one of you makes a match and sends the invite link; the others open it.

## The range

- **The armory:** the back wall holds every gun and every hack on its own stand. E takes it into your hands, and E
  again fuses it a level up.
- **The dummies:** stand, strafe or crouch (the full stop key), and reset them with Y. They can shoot back (the Play
  tab's Range box).
- **The Run and THE CHAIN:** timed movement courses through the lit gates behind you, with your best times kept.
- **The movement lab:** a block of floors, walls and gaps for every move.
- **The superglide trainer, the flick drill and the spray wall:** the timing bar on every mantle, thirty targets in a
  cone against the clock, and your spray against each gun's pattern.

## Settings

- **Graphics:** Competitive is the fastest, Balanced adds the city's full detail, High adds everything. The FPS counter
  is in the top right.
- **Time of day:** golden hour (the start), the neon night or a hazy day (F8 swaps golden hour and the hazy day).
- **Your soldier and loadouts:** the Loadouts tab: your soldier's colours and pieces, your guns, your hacks and your
  melee weapon.
- **Your banner:** an icon, a frame and a title, shown to whoever you eliminate and when your squad wins.
