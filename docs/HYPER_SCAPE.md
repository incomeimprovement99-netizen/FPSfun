# Hyper Scape: everything it had, what we took, and what we liked and forgot

The owner: *"combine hyperscape elements, list them out and see what we forgot
we liked"*. This is the consolidated list. The per-element gap table with
effort and value is `docs/GAP_ANALYSIS.md` section 3; the sourced numbers are
`docs/RESEARCH_PHASE_11.md` section 14 and `docs/RESEARCH_PHASE_12.md`. Hyper
Scape (Ubisoft, 2020 to 2022) is shut down, so every number here is from
Ubisoft's patch notes as the wiki quotes them, and is marked where it is ours.

## The short answer

We took **one** thing: the crown. We have the movement and the guns from Apex,
and the crown from Hyper Scape. **Nine ideas we wrote down and liked are still
not built**, and three of them are cheap.

## The whole list

## What we forgot, ranked by what it would buy us now

1. **Verticality and no fall damage in the maps** (#8, #9). The owner's own
   complaint about our battle royale — "much less open space", "ziplines,
   jump towers, all that jazz" — *is* this item. Hyper Scape's answer was to
   build upward and remove the punishment for coming down. Cheapest useful
   version: roofs you can fight on, ziplines between them, jump pads up, and
   a mode rule that cancels fall stun.
2. **Game Master events** (#7, about a day). One event every 90 s, the host
   picks or the squad votes. Low gravity and infinite ammo need no new art.
3. **Sector decay ending in the crown** (#3 + #4, about two days). Our five
   places delete one at a time; the last one holds the crown.
4. **Hacks and fusion as a mode** (#1 + #2, three to four days). The big one.
   Two slots, six hacks, duplicates fuse.
5. **Echo** (#5, about a day). Out, you fly, you see, you ping, you wait.

## What we should not take

- **120 HP with regeneration** (#10) and **loot with no attachments** (#11):
  both are good design, both contradict the point of this project, which is
  Apex's numbers. They belong in a mode, not in the game.
- **Crowncast itself** (#7's delivery): there is no audience. The idea
  survives as a host or squad vote; the Twitch half does not.
