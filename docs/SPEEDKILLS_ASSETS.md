# SpeedKills: assets, free now and what $50 could buy

**Written 2026-09-26.** The owner's rule: free assets until the game is built, with up to $50 USD to spend
after that (`docs/PHASE_18_PLAN_SPEEDKILLS.md`, the owner's decisions). This is the list to decide from.

## Free, and worth trying next

- **Quaternius, Sci-Fi Essentials Kit** (quaternius.com/packs/scifiessentialskit.html): 60+ models including
  textured guns, animated screens and crates. FBX, OBJ, glTF. CC0. It is downloaded by hand from itch.io, not by
  a script.
- **Quaternius, Modular Sci-Fi MegaKit** (quaternius.com/packs/modularscifimegakit.html): 270+ modular
  pieces (walls, floors, doors, props) for the insides of the city's towers. CC0.
- **Quaternius, Cyberpunk Game Kit** (quaternius.com/packs/cyberpunkgamekit.html): platforms, turrets and
  modular parts in the right theme. CC0.
- **Kenney, Blaster Kit** (kenney.nl/assets/blaster-kit): 18 sci-fi guns, scopes and grenades, GLB. CC0. It
  downloads by script, but it is a bright toy style (foam darts, purple and orange plastic). For first person
  in a dark city it would need recolouring at least, so the look is the owner's call before any work.

## What $50 could buy, and the catch

- **Synty, POLYGON Sci-Fi Cyber City** (syntystore.com/products/polygon-sci-fi-cyber-city): 700+ assets, 157
  buildings. **$199.99** one-off, four times the budget. The Synty subscription is $30 a month.
- **The catch for any paid pack:** a browser game ships its models to every player, where anyone can save them
  from the page. Many store licences forbid giving out the source assets in a form that can be pulled out. The
  Synty page does not say how it treats WebGL. **Read the licence for web distribution before buying anything.**

## Recommendation

Spend nothing yet. The free CC0 kits above cover gun models, tower interiors and props. The $50 is best kept
for the one thing free packs do worst: a coherent, hand-made look for the ten guns. When the owner has
played, a single small paid pack could do that, checked first for a web-distribution clause.
